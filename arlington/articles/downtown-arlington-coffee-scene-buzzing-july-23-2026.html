Downtown Arlington's Coffee Scene Is Buzzing This Summer

July 23, 2026 • Arlington Pulse • 5 min read

There's something special happening on Division Street.

While the rest of Arlington sweats through another scorching July, downtown's local business corridor has become the coolest place to be—literally and figuratively. From freshly roasted coffee to community-driven events, here's what's brewing in the heart of The American Dream City.

Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters: Where the Magic Happens

Walk into Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters at 400 E Division Street and you'll immediately smell the difference. This family-owned operation doesn't just serve coffee—they roast their own beans right on site, creating a coffeehouse experience that's become a staple for UTA students, remote workers, and caffeine enthusiasts across Tarrant County.

Their cold brew has developed something of a cult following, and for good reason. But what really sets Salter Bros. apart is their commitment to collaboration. Their coffee-beer collaboration with Legal Draft Beer Co. (just steps away) has become a local favorite, while their beans are now an option at UnionWorx and Sugar Bee Sweets Bakery.

The Tipsy Oak: More Than Just a Bar

Just down the street, The Tipsy Oak continues to redefine what a neighborhood gathering place can be. With its sprawling patio and craft cocktail program, it's become the unofficial after-hours office for Arlington's creative class. Their rotating seasonal menu keeps regulars coming back, while the relaxed atmosphere makes it equally suitable for casual meetups or productive work sessions.

Hurtado Barbecue: Texas Tradition, Arlington Style

No conversation about downtown Arlington's food scene is complete without mentioning Hurtado Barbecue. Located conveniently near the Salter Bros. and Legal Draft corridor, this award-winning smokehouse has put Arlington on the Texas BBQ map. Their brisket has earned accolades from critics and locals alike, while their partnership with Salter Bros. shows the collaborative spirit that defines this neighborhood.

Inclusion Coffee: Where Community Meets Creativity

Over at Inclusion Coffee, the focus isn't just on great coffee—it's on building community. Their recent "Live Corndog Portraits" event for First Thursdays exemplifies the playful, inclusive approach that's made them a downtown favorite. With Disney-themed secret menus and regular community events, they've created a space where everyone feels welcome.

Newcomers to Watch: Blue Mint Thai & Rocketbelly

The downtown revitalization shows no signs of slowing. Rocketbelly, Texas' first self-serve boba bar, has been drawing crowds with its innovative approach to bubble tea, while Blue Mint Thai is bringing authentic Thai flavors to the Urban Union development. These new additions join an already impressive lineup that includes Division Brewing and Marquez Bakery.

Why This Matters

What we're witnessing in downtown Arlington isn't just business growth—it's community building. These aren't chain establishments; they're locally owned, community-focused operations that reinvest in our city. When you grab coffee at Salter Bros. or barbecue at Hurtado, you're supporting Arlington families, not distant shareholders.

The summer heat may be intense, but downtown Arlington's local business scene is proving that community-driven commerce can thrive even in the dog days of July.
