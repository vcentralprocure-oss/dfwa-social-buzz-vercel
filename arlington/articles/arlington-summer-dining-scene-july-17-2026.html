Arlington's Summer Dining Scene: 5 Local Restaurants You Need to Try This July

Published July 17, 2026 | Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Daniel on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in Arlington, and there's no better time to explore the incredible local dining scene that's been quietly transforming our city. While tourists flock to the big chains near the stadiums, savvy locals know the real flavor of The American Dream City lives in its independent restaurants.

Hurtado Barbecue

Continues to dominate the local barbecue conversation with their famous "Texas Twinkies" – brisket-stuffed jalapeños wrapped in bacon that have developed a cult following across DFW. Located in the heart of downtown, this spot has become a must-visit for anyone serious about smoked meats.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

Rocketbelly

For those craving something refreshing on these hot July days, Rocketbelly has quickly become Arlington's go-to spot for boba tea and sweet treats. This newcomer to the downtown scene is already drawing lines with their creative flavor combinations and Instagram-worthy presentations.

Visit Rocketbelly →

The Tipsy Oak

Remains the perfect destination for those seeking a relaxed patio experience. With its extensive craft beer selection and elevated pub fare, this Urban Union District favorite has mastered the art of outdoor dining. Their rooftop views and weekend live music make it an essential summer stop.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

Blue Mint Thai

When it comes to international flavors, Blue Mint Thai has Arlington residents raving about authentic Thai cuisine made with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes. This locally-owned gem proves you don't need to drive to Dallas for exceptional Asian fare.

Visit Blue Mint Thai →

Division Brewing

Continues to anchor Arlington's craft beer revolution. With rotating taps featuring experimental brews and food truck partnerships that keep the patio buzzing, it's become a community gathering place where strangers become friends over pints.

Visit Division Brewing →

These five spots represent just a taste of what's happening in Arlington's evolving food scene. Each one brings something unique to the table while contributing to the local character that makes our city special.
