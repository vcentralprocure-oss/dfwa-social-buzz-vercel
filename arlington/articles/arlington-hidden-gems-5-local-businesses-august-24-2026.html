5 Hidden Gems in Arlington You Need to Discover This Week

📅 August 24, 2026 • 🕒 4 min read • 📍 Arlington, TX

Arlington isn't just the home of AT&T Stadium and Six Flags—it's a city bursting with local flavor, hidden coffee corners, and family-run gems that deserve your attention. As summer winds down and the back-to-school routine settles in, there's no better time to explore the local businesses that make The American Dream City truly special.

We've rounded up five local spots that are flying under the radar but delivering experiences worth seeking out. From craft coffee roasters to community-focused dining, these businesses represent the heart of Arlington's entrepreneurial spirit.

☕ Arlington Coffee Roasters

Arlington Coffee Roasters

Downtown's first locally-owned coffee roastery and tasting room. They roast their beans right in the shop, ensuring every cup is as fresh as it gets. The industrial-chic space is perfect for remote work or catching up with friends.

Visit Website →

☕ Nehemiah Coffee Co.

Nehemiah Coffee Co.

More than just great coffee—Nehemiah is on a mission to build community through every cup. Their cozy atmosphere and commitment to quality sourcing make this a must-visit for coffee enthusiasts who care about where their beans come from.

Visit Website →

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

Hurtado Barbecue

A fourth-generation barbecue joint that's putting Arlington on the Texas BBQ map. From perfectly smoked brisket to house-made sausages, Hurtado's combines tradition with innovation in every bite. Don't skip the loaded brisket baked potato.

Visit Website →

🥖 Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

This family-run institution has been serving Arlington for decades. Fresh tortillas made daily, authentic Mexican pastries, and a loyal following that spans generations. Come early—their famous pan dulce sells out fast.

Visit Website →

🍻 The Tipsy Oak

The Tipsy Oak

A craft cocktail lounge and gastropub that's become a downtown staple. With an ever-rotating seasonal menu, local brews on tap, and a rooftop patio perfect for Texas evenings, The Tipsy Oak delivers an elevated experience without the pretension.

Visit Website →

Why Local Matters

When you spend your dollars at local Arlington businesses, you're doing more than just getting great coffee or BBQ—you're investing in your community. These business owners live here, hire here, and give back here. They're your neighbors, and they pour their passion into every product and experience they create.

So this week, skip the chains and discover what makes Arlington's local scene so special. Whether you're a lifelong resident or new to the area, these five gems are waiting to welcome you.
