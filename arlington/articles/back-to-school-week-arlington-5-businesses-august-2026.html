Back-to-School Week in Arlington: 5 Local Businesses to Help You Start Strong

From coffee shops to fuel your mornings to family-friendly dining for busy weeknights, discover the Arlington businesses making back-to-school season smoother.

August 19, 2026 Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Trac Vu on Unsplash

School bells are ringing across Arlington ISD, and families are settling into new routines. Whether you're a parent juggling drop-offs and pickups or a student heading to UTA for the fall semester, these five local businesses are here to make your transition back to the classroom a little easier.

Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters

Nothing says "back to school" quite like that first cup of coffee. Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters has been fueling Arlington mornings since 2016, and their dedication to craft coffee shows in every pour. Located in the heart of downtown, this local roaster sources beans from around the world and roasts them in small batches right here in The American Dream City.

Their cozy atmosphere makes it an ideal spot for students looking to study or parents needing a quiet moment before the chaos begins. Try their signature house blend or ask about their seasonal single-origin offerings. They also sell beans by the bag, so you can bring the Salter Bros. experience home.

Mama Cuca's

When homework piles up and cooking feels impossible, Mama Cuca's delivers the comfort food your family craves. This family-owned gem has been serving authentic Mexican cuisine to Arlington residents for years, building a reputation for generous portions and recipes passed down through generations.

Their enchiladas and fajitas are local legends, but don't overlook their breakfast menu for those early morning fuel-ups. With affordable family meals and a welcoming atmosphere, it's the perfect spot for a post-school celebration or a midweek dinner when you need a break from the kitchen.

Taste Community Restaurant

Located in nearby Fort Worth but beloved by Arlington families, Taste Community Restaurant offers something truly unique: a pay-what-you-can model that ensures everyone in our community has access to quality meals. This nonprofit restaurant serves chef-prepared food in a dignified setting, making it a meaningful choice for families who want to support community-driven dining.

Their focus on fresh, locally-sourced ingredients means the menu changes with the seasons. It's an excellent opportunity to teach kids about food justice while enjoying a delicious meal together.

817 Barbershop

Looking sharp for that first day of school? 817 Barbershop has been keeping Arlington looking fresh with classic cuts and modern styles. This local barbershop combines old-school service with contemporary techniques, creating an experience that's as much about community as it is about grooming.

The skilled barbers here know how to work with all hair types and ages, making it a one-stop shop for the whole family. Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are recommended during the busy back-to-school rush.

Cafe Americana

For those afternoons when you need a pick-me-up between school and evening activities, Cafe Americana delivers. This local favorite serves up espresso drinks, smoothies, and light bites in a relaxed setting that's perfect for both quick stops and longer stays.

Their friendly staff and consistent quality have made them a staple in the Arlington coffee scene. Whether you're grabbing a latte on the way to campus or treating the kids to smoothies after school, Cafe Americana hits the spot.

Start the School Year Right

Back-to-school season is about more than just supplies and schedules. It's about building routines that support your family's wellbeing and connecting with the community that makes Arlington special. These local businesses represent the best of what The American Dream City has to offer: quality, community, and genuine care for their customers.

As you navigate the busy weeks ahead, remember that supporting local isn't just good for the economy, it's good for your soul. Here's to a successful school year, Arlington!
