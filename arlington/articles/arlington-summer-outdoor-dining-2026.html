Arlington's Best Summer Outdoor Dining Spots: Where to Eat Al Fresco in 2026

By Arlington Pulse | May 25, 2026 | Food & Dining

Photo by Ryan Jacobson on Unsplash

Summer has officially arrived in Arlington, and there's no better way to soak up the Texas sunshine than with a meal on one of our city's incredible outdoor patios. From rooftop views to garden terraces, Arlington's restaurant scene has embraced al fresco dining in a big way.

Whether you're looking for a casual brunch spot, a romantic dinner destination, or a place to catch the game with friends, we've rounded up the best outdoor dining experiences The American Dream City has to offer. Grab your sunglasses and let's dig in.

The Ultimate Outdoor Dining Guide

The Tipsy Oak ⭐ 4.6

Location: 301 E. Abram St, Downtown Arlington

The Tipsy Oak has become a downtown institution for good reason. Their massive patio features twinkling string lights, comfortable seating, and a relaxed vibe that's perfect for everything from casual lunches to date nights. The craft cocktail menu rotates seasonally, and their gourmet burgers have earned a devoted following. Don't miss their weekend brunch on the patio—it's the perfect way to start a lazy Saturday.

Division Brewing ⭐ 4.7

Location: 506 E. Main St, Downtown Arlington

Arlington's first craft brewery has created something special with their outdoor beer garden. The industrial-chic patio features long communal tables, fire pits for cooler evenings, and plenty of shade for those scorching Texas afternoons. With a rotating selection of house-made brews and food trucks regularly parked outside, Division Brewing has mastered the art of casual outdoor dining. Bring your dog—they're pet-friendly!

Visit Website →

Hurtado Barbecue ⭐ 4.8

Location: 205 E. Front St, Downtown Arlington

When Texas Monthly names you one of the best barbecue joints in the state, people pay attention. Hurtado Barbecue's outdoor seating area lets you enjoy their legendary brisket, ribs, and famous Texas Twinkies under the open sky. The covered patio provides relief from the sun while still letting you enjoy that summer breeze. Arrive early—their outdoor tables fill up fast, especially on weekends.

J. Gilligan's Bar & Grill ⭐ 4.4

Location: 400 E. Abram St, Downtown Arlington

An Arlington institution since 1999, J. Gilligan's expansive patio is the place to be during game days and summer evenings. With multiple outdoor TVs, you won't miss a minute of the action while enjoying their famous Irish nachos or a cold beer. The casual, welcoming atmosphere makes it a favorite among UTA students, young professionals, and families alike. Their happy hour deals are some of the best in town.

Babe's Chicken Dinner House ⭐ 4.7

Location: 2301 N. Collins St, Arlington

For a true Texas dining experience, you can't beat Babe's. Their Arlington location features a charming covered porch that feels like dining at a friend's country home. The family-style service means endless fried chicken, chicken-fried steak, and all the fixings. It's the kind of place where you can linger over sweet tea and watch the world go by. Perfect for family gatherings or when you want to treat out-of-town guests to something uniquely Texan.

Summer Dining Tips

Before you head out, here are a few local tips to make the most of your outdoor dining experience:

  • Beat the heat: Most Arlington patios have misters or fans, but aim for early evening (6-7 PM) when the sun starts to set and temperatures drop.
  • Make reservations: Popular spots like Hurtado and The Tipsy Oak fill up quickly on weekends. Call ahead or use their online booking systems.
  • Check for live music: Many downtown Arlington restaurants feature local musicians on their patios during summer evenings. Division Brewing and J. Gilligan's regularly host acoustic sets.
  • Bring sunscreen: Texas sun is no joke, even on covered patios. A little SPF goes a long way during those long, leisurely lunches.

More to Explore

Beyond our top picks, Arlington's outdoor dining scene continues to grow. Keep an eye on the Urban Union District, where new restaurants are opening with patio-first designs. The city's commitment to walkable, outdoor-friendly spaces means we're seeing more al fresco options than ever before.

Whether you're a longtime resident or just visiting, summer is the perfect time to experience Arlington's culinary scene in the open air. From craft breweries to barbecue legends, there's a patio with your name on it.
