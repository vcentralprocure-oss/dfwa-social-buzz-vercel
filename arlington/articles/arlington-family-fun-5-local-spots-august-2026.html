Arlington Family Fun: 5 Local Spots Making Memories This August

August 5, 2026 · Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Francisco Rioseco on Unsplash

Summer is winding down, but there's still plenty of time to make memories with your family in Arlington. Before the school bell rings and schedules get hectic, take advantage of these local gems that are keeping the summer spirit alive.

Whether you're looking for outdoor adventure, cultural experiences, or indoor entertainment, Arlington has something for every family. Here are five local spots worth visiting before summer fades.

River Legacy Parks

River Legacy Parks

This 1,300-acre oasis along the Trinity River is Arlington's best-kept secret for family outdoor fun. With miles of hiking and biking trails, picnic areas, and a living science center, River Legacy offers endless exploration opportunities. The park's paved trails are stroller-friendly, making it perfect for families with little ones. Pack a lunch and spend the day surrounded by nature without leaving the city.

Visit River Legacy Parks →

Veterans Park

Veterans Park

When the Texas heat cranks up, Veterans Park's splash pad becomes the place to be. This recently upgraded park features interactive water features that keep kids cool and entertained for hours. The adjacent playground and sports fields mean there's something for everyone. It's the kind of place where parents can relax on shaded benches while kids burn off that endless summer energy.

Explore Arlington Parks →

Arlington Museum of Art

Arlington Museum of Art

For families looking to beat the heat indoors, the Arlington Museum of Art offers cultural enrichment without the crowds of Dallas or Fort Worth. Their rotating exhibitions feature works that appeal to all ages, and the museum's downtown location puts you within walking distance of several family-friendly restaurants. Check their calendar for special family programming happening throughout August.

Visit Arlington Museum of Art →

Fielder House Museum

Fielder House Museum

History comes alive at this beautifully preserved 1914 home in the heart of Arlington. The Fielder House offers a glimpse into what life was like in early Arlington, with exhibits that engage curious kids and nostalgic adults alike. The museum grounds include a historic cabin and windmill that make for great family photos. It's a quiet, educational escape that's perfect for a low-key afternoon.

Learn More at Arlington Historical Society →

Levitt Pavilion Arlington

Levitt Pavilion Arlington

Nothing says summer like free outdoor concerts, and Levitt Pavilion delivers 50+ free shows every year. Bring a blanket, pack some snacks, and enjoy live music under the stars. The family-friendly atmosphere makes it a weekly tradition for many Arlington residents. Check their schedule for August performances—there's something different every night.

See the Concert Schedule →

Why Local Matters

Before the summer slips away, make time to explore these local treasures. They're what make Arlington more than just a place to live—they make it a place to call home. Every visit supports organizations that give back to our community and help preserve what makes The American Dream City special.

Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, these spots offer something you won't find anywhere else. So grab the kids, pack some sunscreen, and make the most of these final summer days.
