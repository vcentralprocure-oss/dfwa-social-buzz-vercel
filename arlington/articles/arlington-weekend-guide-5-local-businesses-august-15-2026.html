Your Arlington Weekend Guide: 5 Local Businesses to Explore August 15-16

📅 August 15, 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read

Photo by Courtney Rose on Unsplash

Arlington's downtown scene is buzzing with energy this August weekend, and there's no better time to explore the local businesses that make The American Dream City special. Whether you're a longtime resident or just visiting, these five spots deserve a spot on your weekend itinerary.

Start Your Morning at Arlington Coffee Roasters

Formerly known as Salter Bros., Arlington Coffee Roasters has established itself as Downtown's first locally-owned coffee roastery, distribution center, and tasting room. Located in the Urban Union district, this spot roasts its beans on-site, filling the air with that irresistible aroma that draws coffee lovers from across the city. Grab a pour-over and a pastry to fuel your weekend adventures.

☕ Arlington Coffee Roasters

Downtown's first locally-owned coffee roastery with on-site roasting and a welcoming tasting room in the Urban Union district.

Visit Website →

Experience Dining with Purpose at Taste Community Restaurant

For lunch, head to Taste Community Restaurant, Arlington's innovative "pay-what-you-can" dining experience. This second location of the Fort Worth-based nonprofit is advancing food security while serving chef-prepared meals in a welcoming atmosphere. The restaurant has received thousands of donations and volunteer applications thanks to its viral TikTok presence, and it's become a cornerstone of community hospitality with heart.

🍽️ Taste Community Restaurant

A "pay-what-you-can" dining experience serving chef-prepared meals while advancing food security in our community.

Visit Website →

Shop Local at Deep Dive Vintage

Spend your afternoon browsing the curated finds at Deep Dive Vintage. This addition to Downtown's growing vintage scene offers carefully selected pieces that tell a story. Whether you're hunting for a unique wardrobe addition or the perfect home décor piece, the treasure hunt is half the fun.

🛍️ Deep Dive Vintage

Curated vintage finds in Downtown Arlington. Discover unique wardrobe pieces and home décor with character.

Visit Website →

Grab a Bite at Rodeo Goat

When dinner rolls around, Rodeo Goat brings energy and creative burgers to Abram Street. Known for their inventive toppings and laid-back atmosphere, this popular spot has quickly become a local favorite for casual dining with friends and family.

🍔 Rodeo Goat

Creative burgers and laid-back atmosphere on Abram Street. A local favorite for casual dining.

Visit Website →

Unwind at The Tipsy Oak

Cap off your weekend at The Tipsy Oak, a Division Street staple that perfectly captures Arlington's craft cocktail scene. With its welcoming vibe and creative drink menu, it's the ideal spot to reflect on your weekend of local exploration.

🍸 The Tipsy Oak

Craft cocktails and welcoming atmosphere on Division Street. The perfect spot to unwind after a day of exploring.

Visit Website →

Why Local Matters

These businesses represent just a slice of the 21 new establishments that opened in Downtown Arlington in 2025, contributing to one of the district's most transformative years yet. From the monthly First Thursdays events to the year-round Farmers Market, there's a renewed energy in The American Dream City that's worth experiencing firsthand.

This weekend, skip the chains and discover what makes Arlington's local business community so special. Each of these spots offers something unique, and together they tell the story of a city that's growing while staying true to its roots.
