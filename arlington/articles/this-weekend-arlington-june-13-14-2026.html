This Weekend in Arlington: Live Music, Local Eats & Summer Fun

Your guide to June 13-14, 2026 in The American Dream City — from free concerts at Levitt Pavilion to the best local BBQ and craft beer.

Published June 13, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Summer is in full swing in The American Dream City, and this weekend is packed with events that showcase why Arlington is one of the most vibrant communities in North Texas. Whether you're a longtime resident or just visiting, here's your guide to making the most of June 13-14, 2026.

Free Live Music at Levitt Pavilion

There's no better way to spend a Saturday evening than at Levitt Pavilion in Downtown Arlington. This weekend features free live music under the stars, with local artists taking the stage starting at 7:30 PM. Bring a blanket, grab some takeout from a nearby restaurant, and enjoy the kind of community gathering that makes Arlington special.

Downtown Farmers Market

Start your Saturday morning at the Downtown Arlington Farmers Market (open 8 AM to 1 PM). This weekend's vendors include Stone Oak Farm with their famous heirloom tomatoes, Texas Heritage Meats bringing farm-fresh eggs and pasture-raised chicken, and several local artisans selling handmade crafts. It's the perfect spot to stock up on fresh produce while supporting local farmers.

Local Business Spotlights

This weekend is the perfect time to explore some of Arlington's standout local businesses:

Rocketbelly — Downtown's newest sensation featuring Texas' first self-serve bubble tea bar. Their space-themed restaurant serves out-of-this-world chicken that's become the talk of the town. The famous Rocket chicken crosses traditional tenders with bold flavors you won't find anywhere else.

Division Brewing — Arlington's first craft brewery continues to impress with their rotating tap selection and laid-back patio atmosphere. This weekend they're releasing a new summer saison that's perfect for beating the Texas heat. Grab a flight and settle in for an afternoon of local flavor.

Hurtado Barbecue — Known for their award-winning brisket and legendary Texas Twinkies, Hurtado has become a destination for barbecue lovers across DFW. The line moves fast, and the smoky aroma alone is worth the visit. Pro tip: get there early before they sell out.

The Tipsy Oak — This gastropub has become a downtown staple for good reason. With one of the best patios in Arlington and a menu that elevates pub fare to an art form, it's the ideal spot for weekend brunch or dinner. Their craft cocktail program rivals anything in Dallas or Fort Worth.

Cane Rosso — The highly anticipated pizzeria is now open in Downtown Arlington, bringing their famous Neapolitan-style brick oven pizza to The American Dream City. House-made mozzarella, wood-fired crust with that perfect char, and authentic Italian flavors make this a must-visit.

Sunday Funday at River Legacy

Wrap up your weekend with a visit to River Legacy Parks. This 1,300-acre urban oasis offers miles of hiking and biking trails, picnic areas, and some of the best sunset views in DFW. The Living Science Center is perfect for families, while the more secluded trails offer peaceful escapes for solo adventurers.
