Your Ultimate Guide to July 4th Weekend in Arlington

July 3, 2026

Photo by Ray Hennessy on Unsplash

This Fourth of July, Arlington is the place to be as we celebrate two historic milestones: America's 250th birthday and Arlington's 150th anniversary. From one of the largest Fourth of July parades in Texas to family-friendly races, thrilling fireworks, and a spectacular drone show, the city is packed with patriotic fun all weekend long.

Whether you're a longtime resident or visiting for the holiday, here's your guide to an unforgettable Independence Day celebration in The American Dream City — plus the local businesses making this weekend even more special.

🎆 Light Up Arlington 2026

The celebration continues Sunday, July 5, 2026, with Light Up Arlington 2026, the city's biggest Independence Day event yet. Created to honor both Arlington's 150th anniversary and America's 250th birthday, this special evening in the Entertainment District will feature a breathtaking combination of drone technology, music, and fireworks in a one-of-a-kind visual experience.

  • Parking lots open: 7:00 p.m.
  • Drone and fireworks show begins: 9:30 p.m.
  • Approximate show length: 30 minutes

Fireworks will be launched from the Six Flags parking area, creating spectacular views throughout the Entertainment District. Free parking available at Esports Stadium Arlington & Expo Center and Texas Rangers Parking Lots F, G, M and N.

🎪 Arlington Independence Day Parade

The annual Arlington Independence Day Parade returns on Saturday, July 4, 2026, bringing patriotic pride to Downtown Arlington. Recognized as the largest Independence Day parade in Texas, this beloved tradition features colorful floats, marching bands, community groups, military tributes, and special celebrations.

🏃 Arlington Firecracker 5K

Before the parade steps off, lace up your running shoes for the Arlington Firecracker 5K. Starting at 7 a.m. on July 4, this festive race follows the parade route through Downtown Arlington and begins and ends at the iconic Levitt Pavilion.

🎢 Six Flags Star-Spangled Nights

Head over to Six Flags Over Texas on July 3 and July 4 for Star-Spangled Night presented by M&M's. This annual celebration combines the thrills of world-class rides with a patriotic fireworks display that lights up the Arlington skyline. The 15-minute firework show begins at 10 p.m. both evenings.

⚾ Texas Rangers Baseball

Baseball, hot dogs, and summer days — it doesn't get much more American than that! On July 4 & 5, the Texas Rangers face off against the Detroit Tigers at Globe Life Field. Get there early — the first 10,000 fans will receive fun promotional gear.

🍽️ Where to Eat & Drink This Weekend

Celebrating works up an appetite. Here are five local Arlington businesses ready to fuel your Independence Day weekend:

The Tipsy Oak

Located in the heart of Downtown Arlington at 301 E Front St, The Tipsy Oak is a chef-driven gastropub and icehouse serving craft beer, wine, and full bar options. With live music and a vibrant patio, it's the perfect spot to grab a cold drink after the parade. Visit Website →

Hurtado Barbecue

The Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers! Hurtado Barbecue brings championship-worthy Tex-Mex BBQ to Arlington. Find them inside Globe Life Field at Arlington Eats in Section 101 during every home game, or visit their downtown location for brisket that'll make you proud to be a Texan. Visit Website →

Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery at 506 E. Main St. Division Brewing elevates the beer drinker's experience with unique flavors — from clean, refreshing light ales to malty amber beers and fruity sours. Their outdoor patio is the perfect place to unwind after the fireworks. Visit Website →

Levitt Pavilion

More than just the start/finish line for the Firecracker 5K, Levitt Pavilion Arlington hosts 50+ free outdoor concerts each year. Located in the heart of Downtown, it's where the community comes together for music, culture, and celebration under the stars. Visit Website →

Six Flags Over Texas

The original Six Flags park and a true Arlington icon! Home to 14 world-class coasters and over 100 attractions, Six Flags Over Texas delivers thrills for the whole family. Don't miss their Star-Spangled Nights fireworks show on July 3rd and 4th at 10 p.m. Visit Website →

🎨 Arlington Museum of Art

Open late for Light Up Arlington! The Arlington Museum of Art will be open from 1pm - 9pm on Sunday, July 5, giving you time to explore exhibitions before the 9:30 PM fireworks and drone show lights up the sky right outside their doors.

🏛️ Freedom Fest at the National Medal of Honor Museum

Celebrate America's birthday on Saturday, July 4th – Sunday, July 5th at the National Medal of Honor Museum. Enjoy patriotic festivities, live music, immersive exhibits, family-friendly activities, a special film screening, and classic summertime favorites fresh from the grill!
