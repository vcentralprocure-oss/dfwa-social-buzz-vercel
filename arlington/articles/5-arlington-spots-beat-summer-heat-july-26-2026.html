5 Arlington Spots to Beat the Summer Heat

July 26, 2026 | Arlington Pulse | Local Dining

Summer in Arlington is in full swing, and locals know that when the temperatures climb, it's time to find the perfect spots to stay cool while supporting our vibrant local business community. From refreshing treats to air-conditioned havens with character, The American Dream City has no shortage of places to beat the heat.

This week, we're spotlighting five local businesses that are keeping Arlington cool and thriving. Whether you're looking for a frozen pick-me-up, a craft cocktail, or just a comfortable place to relax away from the sun, these spots deliver the perfect summer experience.

🍧 Kowloon Ice Cream

For a truly unique frozen treat experience, Kowloon Ice Cream brings Asian-inspired flavors to Arlington that you won't find anywhere else. Located in the heart of the city, this local gem serves up handcrafted ice cream with flavors like Vietnamese coffee, Thai tea, and lychee rose. Their rolled ice cream technique isn't just delicious—it's mesmerizing to watch. On a scorching July afternoon, their matcha green tea or black sesame flavors offer the perfect cool-down while supporting a locally-owned business bringing international flair to our community.

🍺 Legal Draft Beer Company

Craft beer enthusiasts have made Legal Draft Beer Company a destination for good reason. This Arlington-based brewery has built a reputation for creative, high-quality brews with clever legal-themed names. Their spacious taproom offers plenty of air-conditioned comfort, and their rotating seasonal selections mean there's always something new to try. The "Presumed Innocent" IPA and "Smoochy" sour are local favorites, but don't miss their summer seasonal releases. With board games, comfortable seating, and a welcoming atmosphere, it's the perfect spot to spend a hot afternoon.

☕ White Rhino Coffee

When you need caffeine but can't handle another hot coffee, White Rhino Coffee has perfected the art of cold brew. With multiple locations across Arlington, this local coffee shop has become a community gathering place for remote workers, students, and coffee lovers alike. Their nitro cold brew is silky smooth and refreshing, while their iced lattes feature house-made syrups that elevate every sip. The comfortable seating and reliable WiFi make it an ideal spot to escape the heat while getting work done or catching up with friends.

🍸 The Tipsy Oak

For craft cocktails in a cool, inviting atmosphere, The Tipsy Oak on West Abram Street delivers an experience that keeps locals coming back. Their frozen cocktail menu is essential summer drinking—creative, refreshing, and perfectly balanced. The spacious interior offers plenty of room to spread out and relax, while their regular events and live music keep the energy lively. Whether you're meeting friends for happy hour or enjoying a weekend evening out, it's a go-to spot for beating the heat in style.

🍽️ Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Sometimes you want a full meal in a comfortable, family-friendly environment. Babe's Chicken Dinner House has been an Arlington institution for years, serving up legendary fried chicken and all the fixings in a cool, welcoming dining room. Their unlimited family-style service means you can relax and enjoy without rushing, and the nostalgic atmosphere makes every visit feel like a special occasion. It's the perfect spot for a leisurely lunch or dinner when you want to escape the summer sun.

Why Supporting Local Matters

Every dollar spent at these Arlington businesses stays in our community, supporting local jobs and helping our neighbors thrive. As we navigate another hot Texas summer, these spots offer more than just relief from the heat—they represent the entrepreneurial spirit that makes Arlington special.

Have a favorite local spot we missed? Let us know—we're always looking for the next great Arlington discovery.

Stay cool out there, Arlington.
