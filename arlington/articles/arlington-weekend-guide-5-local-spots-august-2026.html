Your Arlington Weekend Guide: 5 Local Spots to Explore This August

From legendary fried chicken to scenic nature trails, here's where locals are spending their weekends in The American Dream City

August in Arlington means squeezing every last drop out of summer before the school year kicks into gear. Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, there's no shortage of local gems waiting to be discovered.

Here's your guide to five Arlington businesses and attractions that deserve a spot on your weekend itinerary.

1. Babe's Chicken Dinner House

When locals talk about comfort food in Arlington, Babe's Chicken Dinner House is always part of the conversation. Located at 230 N Center St, this family-friendly spot has earned its reputation for crispy fried chicken and country-cooked meals that taste like Sunday dinner at grandma's house.

The portions are generous, the atmosphere is warm, and their homemade pies have developed something of a cult following. Don't skip the biscuits—they're the real deal.

2. River Legacy Parks

Need a break from the Texas heat? River Legacy Parks offers 1,300 acres of urban oasis along the Trinity River. This isn't your typical city park—it's a thriving ecosystem of bottomland forest, wetlands, and prairie that supports countless species of birds, fish, and mammals.

Bring your bike, lace up your hiking boots, or simply pack a picnic. The nature center provides educational programs for families, and the trails offer a welcome escape without leaving city limits.

3. Arlington Museum of Art

Culture seekers should head straight to the Arlington Museum of Art. Located in the heart of downtown, this dynamic space celebrates creativity through rotating exhibitions that showcase both regional talent and national touring shows.

The museum's commitment to accessible art education makes it a standout cultural institution. Check their calendar for opening receptions—these events offer a chance to meet local artists while supporting Arlington's growing creative community.

4. The Tipsy Oak

For those seeking a laid-back atmosphere with craft cocktails and local brews, The Tipsy Oak delivers. This popular spot has become a gathering place for Arlington residents who appreciate good drinks, better company, and a patio perfect for people-watching.

Their rotating selection of local Texas beers and creative cocktail menu means there's always something new to try. The food menu hits all the right notes for casual dining with elevated touches.

5. Hurtado Barbecue

Texas takes its barbecue seriously, and Hurtado Barbecue has earned its place among the state's best. Located in the heart of Arlington, this award-winning spot serves up brisket, ribs, and house-made sausages that have locals lining up before the doors open.

Their commitment to traditional smoking methods and high-quality ingredients shows in every bite. Come hungry—the portions are Texas-sized.

Plan Your Weekend

Arlington's local business scene continues to grow and evolve, offering residents and visitors alike an impressive variety of dining, recreation, and cultural experiences. These five spots represent just a taste of what The American Dream City has to offer.

Whether you're craving comfort food, seeking outdoor adventure, or looking to support local arts, Arlington delivers. Get out there and explore what makes this city special.
