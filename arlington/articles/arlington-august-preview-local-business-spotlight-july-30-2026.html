Your August Preview: 5 Arlington Businesses to Watch

📅 July 30, 2026 📍 Arlington, TX ⏱️ 4 min read

Photo by Ryan Jacobson on Unsplash

As July winds down and August approaches, Arlington's local business scene is heating up with exciting updates, new offerings, and expanded experiences. From downtown gems to Entertainment District staples, here's your preview of five local businesses making moves this month.

1. Hurtado Barbecue

🔥 Award-Winning Tex-Mex BBQ

Brandon Hurtado's "Tex-Mex" barbecue continues to draw crowds with its unique blend of Austin-style smoking and South Texas spice. Located in the heart of downtown, this spot has become a must-visit for both locals and visitors seeking something beyond traditional Texas BBQ.

Why visit in August: Their summer brisket queso is the perfect way to beat the heat while enjoying world-class barbecue.

Location: 205 E Front St, Arlington, TX 76011

Visit Website →

2. Division Brewing

🍺 Arlington's First Local Brewery

As Arlington's first locally-owned brewery, Division Brewing has been elevating the craft beer scene since day one. Their "Sour Division" program produces some of the most interesting tart, fruity, and mouth-puckering sours in North Texas.

Why visit in August: Extended patio hours and rotating summer sours make this the perfect evening destination.

Location: 506 E Main St, Arlington, TX 76010

Visit Website →

3. The Tipsy Oak

🍔 Chef-Driven Ice House

This family-owned "ice house" themed gastropub has become a downtown staple since 2018. With a chef-driven menu featuring everything from pork belly poutine to Korean BBQ burgers, plus a dog-friendly patio with live music, it's the kind of place that makes you want to linger.

Why visit in August: Their expanded summer cocktail menu features refreshing options perfect for hot Texas evenings.

Location: 301 E Front St, Arlington, TX 76011

Visit Website →

4. Nehemiah Coffee Co.

☕ Downtown's Coffee Sanctuary

Step into Nehemiah Coffee Co. and you'll immediately understand why locals call it their "third place." With natural light streaming through the windows, the aroma of freshly roasted beans, and a community-focused atmosphere, it's the perfect spot to start your day.

Why visit in August: Their iced lavender honey latte has become the unofficial drink of Arlington summers.

Location: 505 E Lamar Blvd, Arlington, TX 76011

Visit Website →

5. Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

🌮 36 Years of Tradition

A true Arlington institution, Marquez Bakery has been serving up authentic Mexican food and fresh tortillas since 1990. Their handmade flour tortillas are the stuff of local legend—soft, pillowy, and made fresh daily.

Why visit in August: Beat the morning rush and grab a dozen warm tortillas for your weekend breakfast tacos.

Location: 1730 E Division St, Arlington, TX 76011

Visit Website →

Support Local This August

These five businesses represent just a slice of what makes Arlington special. Each one has a story, a family behind it, and a commitment to making The American Dream City a little more unique.

As we head into August, make it a point to explore these local favorites. Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, there's always something new to discover in Arlington.
