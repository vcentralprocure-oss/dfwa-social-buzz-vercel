Arlington's Late Summer Dining Scene: 5 Local Restaurants to Visit Before Fall

August 22, 2026 • By Arlington Pulse • 6 min read

Photo by Trac Vu on Unsplash

Summer may be winding down, but Arlington's restaurant scene is just heating up. As August draws to a close and the first hints of fall begin to appear, local chefs and restaurant owners are serving up their best dishes of the season. From outdoor patios perfect for golden hour dining to air-conditioned havens where you can escape the Texas heat, these five Arlington restaurants deserve a spot on your must-visit list before autumn arrives.

Whether you're planning a date night, a family dinner, or just looking for an excuse to skip cooking tonight, The American Dream City's diverse culinary landscape has something for every craving. Here are five local spots making waves this late summer.

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

Hurtado Barbecue

Address: 205 E Front St, Arlington, TX 76010

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands in a league of its own. This fourth-generation family operation has transformed a historic building in downtown Arlington into a temple of smoked meats that draws crowds from across the DFW metroplex. Their brisket—smoked low and slow over post oak—has earned accolades from barbecue purists and casual diners alike.

But Hurtado is more than just great meat. Their creative sides, including elote-inspired corn and house-made pickles, complement the smoky flavors perfectly. The spacious patio, complete with string lights and local craft beer on tap, makes it an ideal spot for those lingering summer evenings when you want to savor every bite outdoors.

Don't miss: The brisket tacos and their rotating seasonal sides that showcase Texas produce at its peak.

🍷 The Tipsy Oak

The Tipsy Oak

Address: 301 E Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

If Arlington had a living room, it would look a lot like The Tipsy Oak. This neighborhood gastropub has become the go-to gathering spot for locals who want craft cocktails, elevated comfort food, and a vibe that manages to be both trendy and welcoming. Located in the heart of downtown, The Tipsy Oak perfectly captures Arlington's blend of small-town charm and big-city sophistication.

Their late summer menu features refreshing cocktails that beat the heat—think cucumber-infused gin drinks and house-made lemonades spiked with local spirits. The outdoor seating area, shaded by mature oak trees, provides a respite from the August sun while still letting you enjoy Arlington's pleasant evenings.

Don't miss: Their weekend brunch and the rotating selection of local craft beers on tap.

🥖 Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

Address: 1730 E Division St, Arlington, TX 76011

For over three decades, Marquez Bakery has been an Arlington institution. This family-run bakery and tortilla factory on Division Street is where locals go when they want authentic Mexican pan dulce, fresh tortillas, and the kind of breakfast tacos that ruin you for all other breakfast tacos. The aroma of baking bread hits you the moment you walk through the door—a sensory experience that never gets old.

Their late summer offerings include seasonal fruit-filled empanadas and refreshing aguas frescas in flavors like watermelon and cucumber. The no-frills dining area fills up quickly on weekend mornings with families, college students, and longtime regulars who've been coming here since the 90s.

Don't miss: The chorizo and egg breakfast tacos on freshly made flour tortillas.

🍺 Division Brewing

Division Brewing

Address: 506 E Main St, Arlington, TX 76010

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery continues to be a cornerstone of the community. Division Brewing, located in a converted industrial space near downtown, has built its reputation on innovative craft beers that range from crushable lagers to experimental sours. Their taproom, with its exposed brick and communal seating, embodies the collaborative spirit of Arlington's growing craft beer scene.

As summer winds down, Division's outdoor beer garden becomes the place to be. Food trucks rotate through regularly, offering everything from wood-fired pizza to Korean BBQ, while live music on weekends creates a festival-like atmosphere. It's the kind of place where you come for one beer and end up staying for three.

Don't miss: Their rotating seasonal releases and the community events that bring Arlington residents together.

🍳 Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Address: 230 N Center St, Arlington, TX 76011

Some restaurants define a city's culinary identity, and for Arlington, Babe's is that restaurant. This legendary fried chicken destination has been serving up crispy, golden perfection since 1981. The rustic, barn-like atmosphere—with its checkered tablecloths and country décor—transports you to a simpler time when dinner was an event and calories didn't count.

The all-you-can-eat family-style service means baskets of fried chicken, bottomless sides like mashed potatoes and creamed corn, and fresh-baked biscuits keep coming until you wave the white flag. It's indulgent, it's unapologetic, and it's quintessentially Texan.

Don't miss: The fried chicken (obviously) and their legendary biscuits with honey.

Savor the Season

These five restaurants represent just a slice of Arlington's vibrant food scene, but they each capture something essential about what makes dining in The American Dream City special. From multi-generational family recipes to innovative craft brewing, Arlington's culinary landscape reflects the diversity and creativity of the community itself.

As the days get shorter and the temperatures (eventually) start to drop, make it a point to visit these local gems. Summer may be ending, but great food is always in season in Arlington.
