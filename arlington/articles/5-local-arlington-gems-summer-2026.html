5 Local Arlington Gems You Need to Visit This Summer

From craft breweries to cozy coffee spots, discover the best local businesses making Arlington's downtown scene thrive

📅 June 29, 2026 📍 Arlington, TX

Photo by Nafinia Putra on Unsplash

Arlington's downtown scene is buzzing with energy this summer, and if you haven't explored the local gems that make this city special, now is the perfect time. From award-winning barbecue to craft breweries and cozy coffee spots, there's something for everyone in The American Dream City.

Inclusion Coffee has quickly become a favorite gathering spot for locals and visitors alike. Located at 101 E Abram Street in the heart of downtown, this welcoming coffee shop serves up expertly crafted beverages in a space designed for connection. Whether you're catching up with friends or need a productive workspace, their commitment to quality and community shines through every cup. Visit Inclusion Coffee →

For those who appreciate the perfect blend of morning energy and evening relaxation, Urban Alchemy offers something truly unique. As downtown Arlington's first locally owned coffee and wine bar, this innovative spot transitions seamlessly from your morning espresso to your evening glass of wine. Located near the UTA campus, it's become a favorite hangout for students, professionals, and anyone seeking that perfect "third place" between work and home. Discover Urban Alchemy →

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands in a league of its own. Housed in a stunning 120-year-old former railroad station on Front Street, this Arlington original serves up Central Texas-style brisket, beef ribs, and smoked specialties that have earned cult status among BBQ enthusiasts. The historic setting adds character to every bite, making it a must-visit for both the food and the atmosphere. Experience Hurtado Barbecue →

Craft beer lovers have made Division Brewing their home base since it opened as Arlington's first locally-owned brewery. Located on East Main Street, this innovative brewery elevates the beer experience with unique flavors ranging from clean, refreshing light ales to hoppy IPAs. The taproom has become a community hub where locals gather to enjoy quality brews and good company. Explore Division Brewing →

Looking for a spot that grows with your day? Downtown's specialty coffee scene has you covered with handcrafted brews made to your taste, paired with delicious bites that go far beyond your typical café fare. These local businesses aren't just places to grab a quick drink—they're community anchors where relationships are built and memories are made.

What makes Arlington's local business scene special is the genuine passion behind each establishment. These aren't corporate chains; they're dreamers, risk-takers, and community builders who chose to invest in The American Dream City. When you spend your dollars at these local spots, you're supporting neighbors, creating jobs, and helping build the vibrant community that makes Arlington such a special place to call home.

This summer, skip the chains and discover what makes Arlington's local business scene truly exceptional. Your taste buds—and your community—will thank you.
