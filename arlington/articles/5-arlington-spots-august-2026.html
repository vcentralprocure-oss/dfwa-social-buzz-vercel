5 Arlington Spots You Need to Visit This August

From Division Street classics to downtown newcomers, here's where locals are eating, drinking, and gathering this month

July 31, 2026 Arlington Pulse Staff

Downtown Arlington's vibrant dining scene continues to draw locals and visitors alike. (Photo: Unsplash)

August in Arlington means one thing: finding the best spots to beat the heat while still enjoying everything The American Dream City has to offer. Whether you're a longtime resident or just passing through, these five local businesses are serving up the kind of experiences that keep people coming back.

Division Street Diner: A Breakfast Institution

If you haven't started your morning at Division Street Diner yet, you're missing out on an Arlington tradition. Located at 1800 W Division Street, this beloved spot has been fueling locals with their Country Eggs Benedict and legendary Blueberry Pancakes for years.

The diner's lunch game is just as strong, with burgers, fresh salads, and sandwiches that hit the spot without breaking the bank. It's the kind of place where the staff remembers your order and the coffee never stops flowing.

Division Street Diner

1800 W Division St, Arlington, TX 76012

Classic American breakfast and lunch spot known for generous portions and friendly service.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Downtown Comes Alive

When the sun goes down, The Tipsy Oak is where Arlington gathers. This gastropub-icehouse hybrid on Abram Street has built a reputation for chef-driven menus that elevate pub fare to an art form.

With craft beer on tap, a full bar, and live music regularly gracing their stage, The Tipsy Oak captures everything that makes downtown Arlington special. Their patio is the perfect spot to catch a breeze on those warm August evenings.

The Tipsy Oak

301 E Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

Gastropub featuring craft cocktails, local beers, and live music in the heart of downtown.

Visit Website →

Hurtado Barbecue: Texas-Sized Flavor

No Arlington food roundup would be complete without mentioning Hurtado Barbecue. What started as a passion project has become a must-visit destination for barbecue lovers across North Texas.

Their Texas Twinkies have achieved near-mythical status, and for good reason. But don't sleep on their brisket, ribs, and house-made sides. This is the real deal, right here in Arlington.

Hurtado Barbecue

205 E Front St, Arlington, TX 76010

Award-winning Texas barbecue featuring brisket, ribs, and their famous Texas Twinkies.

Visit Website →

Marquez Bakery: A Taste of Authenticity

For fresh tortillas, authentic Mexican baked goods, and some of the best breakfast tacos in town, Marquez Bakery is the answer. This family-run institution has been serving the Arlington community for decades.

Walk in and the smell of fresh bread hits you immediately. Their flour tortillas are the stuff of local legend, made fresh daily and sold by the dozen to lines of loyal customers.

Marquez Bakery

1730 E Division St, Arlington, TX 76011

Family-run bakery serving fresh tortillas, Mexican pastries, and authentic breakfast tacos.

Visit Website →

Levitt Pavilion: Free Summer Nights

While not a restaurant, Levitt Pavilion deserves a spot on your August must-visit list. This outdoor music venue in the heart of downtown hosts over 50 free concerts every year, and August is prime time for their summer series.

Pack a blanket, grab some takeout from one of the nearby spots on this list, and enjoy live music under the Texas sky. It's the kind of community experience that makes Arlington feel like home.

Levitt Pavilion

100 W Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

Outdoor music venue presenting 50+ free concerts annually in downtown Arlington.

Visit Website →

Why August Matters for Local Business

Supporting Arlington businesses isn't just about good food and entertainment, it's about keeping our community vibrant and unique. Every dollar spent at a local spot like Division Street Diner or Marquez Bakery stays in Arlington, supporting jobs and keeping our city special.

As the summer winds down and we head into fall, now is the perfect time to revisit old favorites or discover a new go-to spot. These five businesses represent the best of what Arlington has to offer.
