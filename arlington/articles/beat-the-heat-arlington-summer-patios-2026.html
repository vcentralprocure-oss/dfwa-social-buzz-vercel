Beat the Heat: Arlington's Best Summer Patios for Outdoor Dining & Drinks

From craft breweries to rooftop hideaways, discover where locals go to soak up the sun (and the vibes) this summer

Published June 6, 2026 · Arlington Pulse

Summer has officially arrived in Arlington, and there's no better way to enjoy those long, sunny evenings than with a cold drink in hand on a beautiful patio.

Whether you're looking for craft cocktails, local brews, or just a chill spot to catch up with friends, Arlington's outdoor dining scene has something for everyone. Here's your guide to the best patios in The American Dream City this summer.

The Tipsy Oak: Downtown's Patio Paradise

If there's one patio that defines Arlington's outdoor dining scene, it's The Tipsy Oak. Located in the heart of Downtown Arlington, this local favorite has become the go-to spot for craft cocktails, elevated pub fare, and live music under the Texas sky.

The sprawling patio features plenty of shaded seating, string lights that create magic as the sun goes down, and a laid-back atmosphere that keeps locals coming back week after week. Their weekend live music lineup is the perfect backdrop for sipping one of their signature cocktails.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

Division Brewing: Craft Beer & Open Air

For craft beer enthusiasts, Division Brewing is a must-visit. This downtown brewery has built a reputation for innovative small-batch brews and a welcoming community vibe that makes everyone feel like a regular.

Their outdoor seating area is the perfect place to sample their rotating taps while enjoying the breeze. From hazy IPAs to crisp lagers, there's always something new to discover. The pet-friendly patio means your four-legged friends can join the fun too.

Visit Division Brewing →

Hurtado Barbecue: Smoked Meats & Summer Nights

Texas summers demand great barbecue, and Hurtado Barbecue delivers. This local gem has earned a cult following for their perfectly smoked brisket, tender ribs, and all the classic sides you crave.

Their outdoor seating lets you enjoy that authentic Texas BBQ experience under the open sky. Grab a tray of meats, find a picnic table, and prepare for a feast that'll have you planning your next visit before you even finish.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

Wild Acre Restaurant & Beer Garden

Wild Acre brings a true beer garden experience to Arlington. With plenty of outdoor seating, lawn games, and a rotating selection of house-made brews, it's the kind of place where afternoons turn into evenings before you know it.

The casual atmosphere makes it perfect for groups, date nights, or solo adventures. Their food menu complements the beer perfectly, with shareable plates and hearty entrees that satisfy after a day in the Texas heat.

Visit Wild Acre →

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge: Tropical Escape

Looking for something a little different? 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge transports you to a tropical paradise without leaving Arlington. Their themed patio is decorated with tiki torches, palm fronds, and island vibes that make every visit feel like a vacation.

The creative cocktail menu features rum-forward drinks served in festive glassware, and the outdoor seating area is the perfect spot to unwind with friends. It's a unique addition to Arlington's patio scene that stands out from the crowd.

Visit 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge →

Plan Your Patio Hopping Adventure

Arlington's outdoor dining scene is more vibrant than ever, with new spots opening regularly and established favorites continuing to raise the bar. This summer, make it a mission to explore these patios and discover your new favorite hangout.

Whether you're a longtime local or new to the area, there's always something fresh to experience in The American Dream City. Grab your sunglasses, bring your appetite, and get ready to patio hop your way through summer 2026.
