5 Arlington Restaurants You Need to Try This Summer

From award-winning Tex-Mex BBQ to craft breweries with perfect patio vibes — your guide to Arlington's best summer dining spots.

Published July 2, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Summer in Arlington means one thing: it's time to get out and explore the incredible food scene that's been quietly building momentum in The American Dream City. From award-winning barbecue to craft breweries with the perfect patio vibes, here's where locals are eating right now.

Hurtado Barbecue — Where Tex-Mex Meets Smoke

If you haven't experienced Hurtado Barbecue yet, you're missing out on one of Texas's fastest-growing BBQ success stories. What started as pop-ups and food trucks has exploded into a downtown Arlington institution that's now serving Rangers fans at Globe Life Field.

Their secret? Tex-Mex barbecue that doesn't apologize for being bold. The brisket is Central Texas-style perfection, but it's the birria tacos and brisket tostadas that keep people coming back. Order the El Jefe if you're hungry — it's a mountain of meat that feeds two (or one very committed BBQ enthusiast).

Division Brewing — Craft Beer on Front Street

Right in the heart of downtown, Division Brewing has become the unofficial community living room for Arlington's craft beer lovers. With a rotating tap list featuring everything from hazy IPAs to crisp lagers, there's always something new to try.

The outdoor seating area is where the magic happens on summer evenings. Bring your dog, bring your friends, and settle in for a laid-back night that feels distinctly Arlington.

The Tipsy Oak — Patio Paradise

When the weather's nice (and let's be honest, that's most days in Arlington), The Tipsy Oak is where you want to be. This local favorite has built its reputation on having one of the best patios in the city — spacious, dog-friendly, and always buzzing with energy.

The menu covers all the bases: burgers, sandwiches, and shareable plates that pair perfectly with their craft cocktail selection. It's casual dining done right.

Cane Rosso — Neapolitan Pizza Done Right

For pizza purists, Cane Rosso is worth the trip. Their wood-fired Neapolitan pies have earned a cult following across Texas, and the Arlington location delivers the same quality that made them famous.

The crust is the star — blistered, chewy, and authentically Italian. Whether you go classic Margherita or venture into their creative seasonal specials, you're in for a treat.

Rodeo Goat — Burgers with Personality

Sometimes you just need a really good burger. Rodeo Goat answers that call with creative, over-the-top creations that don't skimp on flavor. The "Chaca Oaxaca" with chorizo and queso is a local favorite, but the menu goes deep with options for every taste.

Pair your burger with one of their craft beers or house-made cocktails, and you've got the perfect casual dinner spot.

Your Summer Dining Adventure Starts Here

Arlington's food scene has never been more exciting. Whether you're a longtime resident or just passing through, these five spots represent the best of what The American Dream City has to offer.
