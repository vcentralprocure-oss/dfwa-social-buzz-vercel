Arlington's Late Summer Hotspots: 5 Local Businesses to Visit Before Fall

From award-winning barbecue to craft cocktails, discover the local gems making the most of summer's final weeks in The American Dream City.

August 18, 2026 Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Max Fray on Unsplash

Summer is winding down, but Arlington's local business scene is still heating up. Whether you're looking to savor the season or get a head start on fall planning, these five local spots are serving up the best of The American Dream City.

Hurtado Barbecue

If you haven't experienced Hurtado Barbecue yet, consider this your final summer mission. This Michelin-listed spot on East Front Street has redefined what barbecue means in Arlington. Brandon Hurtado brings Central Texas smoking techniques together with South Texas spice for a Tex-Mex barbecue fusion that's earned national attention.

The brisket here is the main event, but don't skip the birria tacos. They're not a novelty add-on, they're part of the core identity. The patio seating makes it perfect for those warm August evenings when you want great food without the formality.

The Tipsy Oak

Just down the street, The Tipsy Oak has become downtown Arlington's go-to gathering spot. This gastropub-icehouse hybrid delivers chef-driven comfort food alongside an impressive craft beer selection and creative cocktails.

With live music on weekends and a spacious patio, it's the kind of place where you can linger for hours. Their rotating seasonal menu means there's always something new to try, making repeat visits a necessity rather than just a nice idea.

Division Brewing

Arlington's original owned and operated brewery, Division Brewing, continues to be a cornerstone of the local craft beer scene. Located in the heart of downtown, this brewery has been serving hand-crafted beers since 2016.

The taproom offers a rotating selection of seasonal brews alongside their flagship favorites. It's the kind of place where regulars know each other by name and newcomers are welcomed like old friends. Grab a flight and explore what's on tap before summer fades.

Nehemiah Coffee Co.

For those early mornings or afternoon pick-me-ups, Nehemiah Coffee Co. delivers some of the best specialty coffee in Arlington. This local roaster has built a loyal following through quality beans and genuine community connection.

Their downtown location makes it an ideal stop before exploring the rest of the area. Whether you're grabbing a pour-over to go or settling in with a latte and laptop, the atmosphere strikes that perfect balance between productive and relaxed.

Marquez Bakery

No list of Arlington essentials would be complete without Marquez Bakery. This family-run institution has been serving fresh tortillas, authentic Mexican pastries, and made-from-scratch meals for generations.

The tortillas alone are worth the trip, still made using traditional methods that produce that unmistakable texture and flavor. Grab a dozen to take home, or stay for breakfast and experience why locals have been coming here for decades.

Make the Most of What's Left

With school back in session and Labor Day approaching, these final weeks of summer deserve to be savored. Each of these businesses represents what makes Arlington special: local owners who care about their craft and their community.

Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, there's never been a better time to explore what The American Dream City has to offer. Support local, eat well, and make these last days of summer count.
