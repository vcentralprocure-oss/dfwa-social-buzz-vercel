This Week in Arlington: Free Concerts, New Coffee Spots & Summer Nights

Your guide to June 18-24, 2026 — from Levitt Pavilion shows to downtown's newest cafés and the best patios for hot summer evenings.

June 18, 2026 • Arlington Pulse

Summer is in full swing in The American Dream City, and this week brings a perfect mix of free outdoor entertainment, exciting new business openings, and plenty of ways to beat the heat. Whether you are a longtime local or just passing through, here is everything you need to know about what is happening in Arlington right now.

Free Music Under the Stars at Levitt Pavilion

The Levitt Pavilion's 2026 summer concert series is hitting its stride, and this weekend promises some memorable performances. The outdoor venue in downtown Arlington continues its tradition of bringing world-class entertainment to the city completely free of charge.

Saturday, June 20 marks the 5th Annual Juneteenth Celebration, featuring live music, community gatherings, and special performances that honor this important day in American history. The Levitt Pavilion has become the heart of Arlington's cultural scene, hosting more than 50 free concerts and events throughout the year.

Grab a blanket, pack some snacks, and enjoy an evening under the Texas stars. The lawn opens early, so come stake out your spot and make an evening of it.

New Faces in Downtown Arlington

Downtown Arlington's revitalization continues with several exciting new businesses opening their doors. If you have not been downtown lately, you are missing out on some fresh additions to the local scene.

Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters is now officially open in the Urban Union district, bringing downtown's first locally-owned coffee roastery, distribution center, and tasting room. This is a game-changer for coffee lovers who want to experience freshly roasted beans without leaving the city.

Also new to the neighborhood is Arwa Coffee, Texas' first Yemeni coffee shop, now serving at 200 E. Abram St. After making waves in Richardson, this unique café has brought its traditional Yemeni coffee preparations to Arlington, offering something truly different from the usual coffee chain experience.

Coming soon, keep an eye out for Blue Mint Thai, a locally-owned Thai restaurant, and Rocketbelly, a boba shop serving refreshing drinks and sweet treats. Downtown's food scene just keeps getting better.

Where to Eat and Drink This Week

When the Texas heat hits, Arlington locals know exactly where to go. Here are five spots that are perfect for summer dining and drinks:

The Tipsy Oak

This ice-house-style gastropub in the Urban Union district features a massive patio, craft beer selection, and live music on weekends. The chef-driven menu goes way beyond typical bar food, and the outdoor seating is perfect for summer evenings.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery remains a cornerstone of the local beer scene. With a rotating tap list and a laid-back atmosphere, it is the ideal spot to cool down with a cold one after work.

Hurtado Barbecue

What started as a pop-up serving hungry brewery-goers has become one of the most talked-about BBQ spots in DFW. Brandon and Hannah Hurtado have created something special with their Tex-Mex influenced smoked meats. The brisket tacos alone are worth the trip.

Daydream

This unique combination romance bookstore and coffee shop has quickly become a downtown favorite. Sip a latte while browsing the shelves — it is like stepping into a Hallmark movie, but better because it is real and right here in Arlington.

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge

When you need to escape the Texas heat, this tropical-themed spot serves up frozen cocktails that transport you straight to island time. The tiki drinks are strong, the atmosphere is fun, and the air conditioning is cranked.

Weekend Events Worth Checking Out

Beyond the Levitt Pavilion concerts, there is plenty happening around town this weekend. The Downtown Arlington Farmers Market continues every Saturday morning, offering farm-fresh produce, handmade crafts, and a chance to support local vendors.

For those who love classic cars, the downtown area regularly hosts car shows and cruise nights that bring out some beautiful vintage rides. Even if you are not a car person, the atmosphere is electric and the people-watching is top-notch.

Summer Nights in The American Dream City

There is something magical about Arlington in the summer. The long evenings, the buzz of activity downtown, the smell of barbecue wafting through the air — it is a season that reminds you why so many people choose to call this city home.

Whether you are catching a free concert, trying a new coffee shop, or just enjoying a patio dinner with friends, this week offers plenty of reasons to get out and explore. The American Dream City is alive and thriving, and there is never been a better time to be here.
