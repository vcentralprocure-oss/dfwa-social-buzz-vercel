Arlington's Summer Dining Guide: 5 Local Spots to Beat the Heat

July 25, 2026 | Arlington Pulse | Local Dining

When the Texas sun is blazing and the temperatures climb into the triple digits, Arlington locals know exactly where to go. Forget staying cooped up inside—The American Dream City has plenty of spots where you can enjoy great food, refreshing drinks, and that unbeatable summer vibe while staying cool.

From craft breweries with expansive patios to local cafes serving up frozen treats, we've rounded up five must-visit local businesses that are perfect for your summer dining adventures. Grab your friends, bring your appetite, and discover why Arlington's food scene is heating up even as the temperatures rise.

🍺 Division Brewing

Located in the heart of Downtown Arlington, Division Brewing has become a community staple for craft beer enthusiasts. Their taproom features rotating seasonal brews perfect for summer sipping, and the laid-back atmosphere makes it an ideal spot to unwind after a long week. With both indoor and outdoor seating options, you can enjoy your pint however you prefer. Don't miss their summer ale series—light, crisp, and exactly what you need on a hot July afternoon.

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

If you think barbecue is just for fall, Hurtado Barbecue will change your mind. This award-winning spot on Division Street serves up some of the best brisket in Texas, and their indoor dining area is perfectly climate-controlled for those scorching summer days. The jalapeño cheddar sausage is a local favorite, and their house-made sides complement the smoky meats perfectly. Pro tip: arrive early on weekends—this place draws crowds for a reason.

🍹 The Tipsy Oak

For craft cocktails and a lively atmosphere, head to The Tipsy Oak on West Abram Street. This local gem has built a reputation for creative drinks and a welcoming vibe that keeps locals coming back. Their frozen cocktail menu is a summer essential, and the spacious patio features misters to keep you cool while you enjoy your evening. With regular live music and events, it's the perfect spot to gather with friends and make the most of those long summer nights.

🧋 Rocketbelly

Texas' first self-serve boba bar has landed in Downtown Arlington, and Rocketbelly is out of this world. This family-owned spot combines refreshing bubble tea with their famous "Rocket Chicken"—a unique cross between traditional tenders and something entirely their own. The space-themed interior is Instagram-worthy, and the customizable boba drinks are the perfect way to cool down. Kids and adults alike will love creating their own flavor combinations.

☕ Nehemiah Coffee Co.

Sometimes you need a caffeine fix even when it's 100 degrees outside. Nehemiah Coffee Co. has you covered with their excellent cold brew and iced coffee selections. This community-focused café on South Cooper Street is more than just a coffee shop—it's a gathering place where locals connect, work remotely, and enjoy quality beverages in a comfortable setting. Their summer menu features refreshing iced teas and seasonal cold brews that hit the spot on even the hottest days.

Summer Dining Tips for Arlington

When exploring Arlington's dining scene this summer, keep these tips in mind:

  • Call ahead for weekend reservations—popular spots fill up fast
  • Many restaurants offer happy hour specials during the week
  • Check social media for daily specials and limited-time summer menu items
  • Don't forget to support local—every dollar spent at these businesses stays in our community

Whether you're a longtime resident or new to The American Dream City, these five local spots showcase the best of Arlington's growing food scene. From award-winning barbecue to innovative bubble tea, there's something for every palate and preference.
