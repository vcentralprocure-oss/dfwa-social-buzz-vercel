5 Arlington Coffee & Brew Spots Brewing Up Something Special This Summer

From award-winning Yemeni coffee to craft brews, discover the local gems defining Arlington's vibrant drink scene.

July 1, 2026 | Arlington Pulse | Local Business Spotlight

Photo by Toa Heftiba on Unsplash

Summer in Arlington means one thing: finding the perfect spot to cool off with a great drink in hand. Whether you're a coffee connoisseur, craft beer enthusiast, or just looking for a new local hangout, Arlington's downtown scene has something brewing for everyone.

Nehemiah Coffee Co. – Where Community Meets Craft

Step into Nehemiah Coffee Co. and you'll immediately feel the difference. Located at 505 E Lamar Blvd, this locally-owned gem has become a cornerstone of Arlington's coffee culture. With natural light streaming through large windows and the rich aroma of freshly roasted beans in the air, Nehemiah offers more than just caffeine—it offers connection.

Their breakfast and brunch menu has locals raving, and the health score of 94 shows their commitment to quality. Whether you're grabbing a quick pour-over before work or settling in for a long laptop session, Nehemiah delivers that perfect neighborhood coffee shop vibe.

Arwa Yemeni Coffee – A Taste of Tradition

Texas' first Yemeni coffee shop has made its way to Arlington, and it's turning heads. Arwa Yemeni Coffee, located at 200 E. Abram St., brings centuries-old coffee traditions to The American Dream City.

This multi-award winning spot specializes in authentic Yemeni coffee using traditional brewing methods you won't find anywhere else in town. The welcoming community space invites you to slow down and savor flavors that have been perfected over generations. It's not just coffee—it's a cultural experience.

Inclusion Coffee – Specialty Brews Downtown

Nestled in the heart of downtown at 101 E Abram St, Inclusion Coffee lives up to its name by creating a space where everyone feels welcome. This specialty coffee shop has quickly become a go-to for Arlington residents seeking superior coffee in a vibrant atmosphere.

Beyond the excellent espresso drinks and pour-overs, Inclusion also hosts events, pop-ups, and even private parties. Looking for a unique venue for your next gathering? Their team can help make it memorable.

Division Brewing – Arlington's First Local Brewery

When the Texas heat hits, sometimes you need something a little stronger than coffee. Enter Division Brewing at 506 E. Main St.—Arlington's first locally-owned brewery and a true pioneer in the city's craft beer scene.

Division elevates the beer drinker's experience with unique flavors ranging from clean, refreshing light ales to hoppy India pale ales. Their downtown location has become a gathering place for locals and visitors alike, complete with trivia nights and live music. It's the perfect spot to unwind after exploring Arlington's entertainment district.

Hurtado BBQ – The Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers

Okay, so Hurtado BBQ isn't technically a coffee shop or brewery, but no Arlington summer guide is complete without mentioning this local legend. Starting from humble pop-up beginnings in 2018, Brandon and Hannah Hurtado have built an explosive success story with their original downtown Arlington location.

Named the Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers, Hurtado serves up championship-worthy Tex-Mex BBQ that draws crowds from across DFW. The El Jefe plate is a must-try, featuring their signature slow-smoked meats with bold flavors that perfectly capture Texas culinary heritage.

Why Local Matters

These five spots represent more than just great drinks and food—they represent the entrepreneurial spirit that's transforming downtown Arlington. Each business brings something unique to the table, creating a diverse landscape of flavors and experiences that chain establishments simply can't replicate.

So this summer, skip the corporate coffee shops and big-box breweries. Instead, explore what Arlington's local entrepreneurs have built. Your taste buds—and your community—will thank you.
