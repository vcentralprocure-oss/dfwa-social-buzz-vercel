Arlington's Best Summer Patios: Where to Eat & Drink Outside This June

From craft breweries to rooftop hideaways, discover the perfect spots to soak up the Texas sunshine while enjoying great food and drinks

Summer evenings were made for patio dining. Photo by Unsplash.

Summer has officially arrived in Arlington, and there's no better way to enjoy the warm Texas evenings than on a patio with a cold drink in hand. Whether you're looking for a casual spot to catch up with friends, a romantic outdoor dinner, or a lively atmosphere with live music, Arlington's patio scene has something for everyone.

We've rounded up the best outdoor dining spots across The American Dream City, from downtown gems to Entertainment District hotspots. Grab your sunglasses and appetite—here's where to eat and drink outside this summer.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Craft Cocktails Meet Backyard Vibes

If you haven't experienced The Tipsy Oak yet, you're missing out on one of Arlington's most beloved patio destinations. Located in the heart of downtown, this gastropub has built a reputation for creative cocktails and a backyard atmosphere that feels like a friend's epic cookout—if your friend happened to be a master mixologist.

Their expansive patio features plenty of shaded seating, string lights that create magic as the sun goes down, and a laid-back vibe that keeps locals coming back. The menu balances elevated pub fare with Texas classics, and their weekend brunch on the patio has become something of a local institution.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

Division Brewing: Craft Beer Paradise with Outdoor Seating

For craft beer enthusiasts, Division Brewing is practically a pilgrimage site. As Arlington's first craft brewery, they've been pouring exceptional beers since 2016, and their outdoor seating area is the perfect place to sample their latest creations.

The brewery's patio buzzes with energy on weekend afternoons, filled with groups sharing flights, families enjoying the relaxed atmosphere, and beer lovers debating the merits of IPAs versus stouts. They frequently host food trucks, live music, and community events that make every visit feel like a celebration.

Visit Division Brewing →

Hurtado Barbecue: Award-Winning Smoked Meats Al Fresco

Texas summers and barbecue go together like brisket and pickles. Hurtado Barbecue has earned national recognition for their smoked meats, and their outdoor seating lets you enjoy that legendary brisket, ribs, and sausage in the open air.

The patio fills up fast on weekends—and for good reason. There's something special about digging into a plate of perfectly smoked meat while the smell of post oak drifts through the air. Arrive early for the best selection, and don't skip the house-made sides.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

Wild Acre Brewing Company: A Patio with Personality

Another standout in Arlington's craft beer scene, Wild Acre Brewing Company offers a patio experience that's become a favorite among locals. The brewery's commitment to quality shows in every pint, and their outdoor space provides plenty of room to spread out and stay a while.

With regular events, rotating food trucks, and a dog-friendly policy, Wild Acre has created the kind of community gathering place that makes Arlington feel like home. Their seasonal releases are always worth checking out, and the patio is the perfect spot to enjoy them.

Visit Wild Acre Brewing →

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge: Escape to the Islands

When the Texas heat has you dreaming of tropical breezes, 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge delivers. This tiki-themed bar brings island vibes to Arlington with creative rum cocktails, Polynesian-inspired decor, and a patio that feels like a mini vacation.

The outdoor area features thatched roofs, tiki torches, and enough personality to transport you straight to the South Pacific. Their signature drinks are as fun to look at as they are to drink, and the patio atmosphere makes every visit feel like a celebration.

Visit 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge →

Planning Your Patio Crawl

With so many great options, you might be tempted to hit multiple spots in one evening. Good news—several of these patios are within easy walking distance of each other in downtown Arlington, making a patio crawl entirely feasible.

Start with barbecue at Hurtado, walk over to Division Brewing for a craft beer, then finish the evening at The Tipsy Oak for cocktails under the string lights. Just remember to pace yourself and designate a driver or use a rideshare service.

Summer Events to Watch For

June is packed with events that pair perfectly with patio weather. The 2026 FIFA World Cup is bringing global attention to Arlington, and many local patios will be buzzing with watch parties and special events. Keep an eye out for:

• Extended happy hours at downtown establishments
• Live music series at Division Brewing and Wild Acre
• Special World Cup viewing events across the city
• Summer cocktail menus launching at The Tipsy Oak and 4 Kahunas

Arlington's patio scene is more than just outdoor seating—it's where the community comes together. Whether you're a longtime local or just visiting, these spots capture the spirit of The American Dream City: welcoming, unpretentious, and always ready to show you a good time.
