Arlington's Craft Beer Scene: Where to Find the Best Local Brews

May 14, 2026 • Arlington Food & Drink Guide • By Arlington Pulse

Photo by Phillip Goldsberry on Unsplash

Arlington's craft beer scene has exploded in recent years, transforming the city into a destination for beer enthusiasts across DFW. What started with a single locally-owned brewery has blossomed into a vibrant community of brewers, pubs, and beer bars that rival anything you'll find in Dallas or Fort Worth.

Whether you're hunting for a hazy IPA, a rich stout, or something completely experimental, Arlington's breweries and craft beer spots deliver. Here's your guide to the best places to grab a pint in the city.

🍺 Division Brewing

Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned and operated craft brewery, Division Brewing has been the cornerstone of the city's beer scene since opening. With over 20 years of combined homebrewing experience, the team here knows how to craft exceptional beer.

Each week, they rotate around 18 freshly-batched craft beers on tap, including innovative flavors like Brut IPA and Imperial Stout with chocolate. The laid-back vibe and knowledgeable staff make this a must-visit for any beer lover.

📍 506 E. Main St, Arlington, TX 76010 | 📞 682-259-7011

🍻 Hooligan's Pub

Hooligan's Pub

Located in the heart of downtown Arlington, Hooligan's Pub has built a stellar reputation as the go-to spot for craft beer aficionados. With over 25 beers on tap that rotate regularly and more than 80 bottle varieties, there's always something new to discover.

The bartenders here are true beer experts who take the time to learn your preferences and recommend the perfect pint. The atmosphere strikes that perfect balance between cool and relaxed—you'll feel like a regular from your very first visit.

📍 310 E Abram St Suite 150, Arlington, TX 76010 | 📞 (817) 274-1232

🍺 Legal Draft Beer Company

Legal Draft Beer Company

Founded on friendship, family, and hard work, Legal Draft Beer Company opened in July 2016 as Arlington's first production brewery. What started when a lawyer and a medical logistics professional quit their day jobs has become a beloved local institution.

Their "Beeranda Rights" philosophy means everyone has the right to drink great beer—and they deliver on that promise. Visit their downtown taproom Thursday through Sunday to sample their latest creations, or find their beers at retailers throughout Texas.

📍 500 E Division St, Arlington, TX 76011 | 📞 (682) 251-8599

🍻 Kool Keg Beer & Margaritas

Kool Keg Beer & Margaritas

A downtown Arlington institution since 1982, Kool Keg has evolved from a keg supplier to a full-service craft beer destination. They offer the largest selection of beer kegs in the DFW Metroplex, plus margarita machines and full-service alcohol catering.

Whether you're planning a party or just want to explore their impressive selection, Kool Keg is a unique Arlington experience that combines old-school charm with modern craft beer culture.

📍 207 S East St, Arlington, TX 76010 | 📞 (817) 461-6949

🌟 Why Arlington's Beer Scene Matters

What makes Arlington's craft beer community special isn't just the quality of the brews—it's the sense of community. These aren't just places to grab a drink; they're gathering spots where neighbors become friends and local culture thrives.

From Division Brewing's neighborhood following to Hooligan's personalized recommendations, Arlington's beer spots prioritize connection over transactions. That's something you can't fake, and it's why locals keep coming back.

🗺️ Plan Your Arlington Beer Crawl

All four of these spots are conveniently located in or near downtown Arlington, making them perfect for a walking beer tour. Start at Division Brewing for a flight of their latest creations, walk over to Hooligan's for a rotating tap selection, then finish at Legal Draft or Kool Keg to discover something new.

Just remember to drink responsibly and designate a driver—or use a rideshare service to get home safely.
