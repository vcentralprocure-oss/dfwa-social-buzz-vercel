Arlington's Summer Dining Scene: 5 Local Restaurants You Need to Try This June

From craft barbecue to rooftop patios, discover the best spots to eat and drink in The American Dream City this summer

June 9, 2026

Photo by Ryan Jacobson on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in Arlington, and there's no better time to explore the city's vibrant dining scene. Whether you're a longtime resident or just visiting for a Rangers game or concert at AT&T Stadium, these five local restaurants are serving up the flavors that make Arlington a true culinary destination.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Craft Meets Comfort

If you haven't experienced The Tipsy Oak yet, you're missing out on one of downtown Arlington's crown jewels. This gastropub-icehouse hybrid has built a reputation for chef-driven comfort food that pairs perfectly with their extensive craft beer selection. The outdoor patio is the place to be on summer evenings, with live music filling the air and locals gathering to unwind after work. Their rotating seasonal menu means there's always something new to try, but regulars know the burgers are not to be missed.

Hurtado Barbecue: Texas Smoke with a Tex-Mex Soul

Hurtado Barbecue has put Arlington on the map for serious barbecue enthusiasts. This isn't your typical Texas smokehouse—here, Central Texas smoking techniques meet bold Tex-Mex flavors in ways that will make you rethink everything you know about barbecue. The brisket is the star of the show, smoked to perfection with that signature bark and tender interior. But don't sleep on their birria tacos, which have developed a cult following among locals. The combination of traditional smoking methods with Mexican culinary influences creates a dining experience that's uniquely Arlington.

Division Brewing: Craft Beer Paradise

When it comes to local craft beer, Division Brewing stands tall as Arlington's original craft brewery. Located in the heart of downtown, this brewery has become a community gathering spot where beer lovers and casual drinkers alike come together. Their taproom features a rotating selection of house-made brews, from crisp lagers perfect for Texas summers to bold IPAs that hop heads crave. The laid-back atmosphere, complete with outdoor seating and frequent food truck partnerships, makes Division Brewing an essential stop on any Arlington food and drink tour.

Rodeo Goat: Burgers Gone Wild

For burger enthusiasts, Rodeo Goat in Arlington delivers creative, over-the-top burger combinations that push the boundaries of what a burger can be. This isn't fast food—it's burger artistry. From the classic cheeseburger done right to wild creations featuring unexpected ingredients, every option on the menu is crafted with care. The casual, energetic atmosphere makes it perfect for family dinners or meetups with friends, and their craft beer selection ensures you'll find the perfect pairing for whatever burger you choose.

Mariano's Hacienda Ranch: A Taste of Texas History

No roundup of Arlington dining would be complete without Mariano's Hacienda Ranch. This legendary establishment has been serving up Tex-Mex classics for decades, and it's where the frozen margarita machine was invented back in 1971. Today, it remains a beloved destination for sizzling fajitas, perfectly seasoned enchiladas, and those famous margaritas that started it all. The historic atmosphere combined with consistently excellent food makes Mariano's a must-visit for anyone wanting to taste a piece of Texas culinary history.

Plan Your Arlington Food Adventure

Whether you're craving smoked meats, craft beer, creative burgers, or legendary Tex-Mex, Arlington's dining scene delivers. These five restaurants represent just a taste of what the city has to offer. Summer is the perfect time to explore—many offer outdoor seating where you can enjoy the warm Texas evenings while savoring some of the best food in the metroplex.
