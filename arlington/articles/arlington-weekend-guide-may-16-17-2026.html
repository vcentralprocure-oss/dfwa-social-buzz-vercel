Your Ultimate Arlington Weekend Guide: What to Do May 16-17, 2026

May 15, 2026 | Arlington Events & Entertainment

Arlington's Entertainment District comes alive on weekends

Looking for something to do this weekend in Arlington? You are in luck. May 16-17 is packed with events, live music, and dining experiences that showcase why our city is the entertainment capital of North Texas.

Live Music and Entertainment

Texas Live! is the place to be this Saturday. Their massive outdoor venue is hosting live music with multiple stages, specialty cocktails, and that unbeatable Arlington energy. Whether you are catching a Rangers game or just soaking in the atmosphere, this entertainment complex delivers every time.

Looking for something more low-key? Downtown Arlington has transformed into a walkable entertainment district with live acoustic sets at several local spots. The area around Abram Street is buzzing with new energy, perfect for an evening stroll between venues.

Where to Eat This Weekend

Arlington is having a serious food moment right now. Here are five local spots worth checking out:

Pappadeaux Seafood Kitchen

New Orleans-style seafood with legendary blackened redfish and gumbo. A local favorite on East Copeland Road.

1304 E Copeland Road, Arlington, TX

Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Legendary family-style fried chicken and all the fixings. Worth the short drive to nearby Roanoke for this Texas tradition.

104 N Oak Street, Roanoke, TX

Jameston's Pub

Elevated pub fare with a local twist near the Entertainment District. Great craft beer selection and weekend brunch.

Near Globe Life Field, Arlington, TX

Mellow Mushroom

Creative pizzas and a laid-back vibe perfect for families, dates, or casual group outings.

South Cooper Street, Arlington, TX

Taverna

Italian classics with Texas hospitality in downtown Arlington. The patio is ideal for weekend people-watching.

Downtown Arlington, TX

Family Fun and Outdoor Activities

River Legacy Parks offers over 1,300 acres of trails, wildlife viewing, and picnic spots. This hidden gem is perfect for morning hikes before the afternoon heat kicks in. The park is free and open from dawn until dusk.

For indoor entertainment, check out the International Bowling Museum near Six Flags. It is an unexpectedly fascinating look at the history of one of America's favorite pastimes, with interactive exhibits that kids and adults both enjoy.

Shopping and Local Finds

The Arlington Farmers Market is in full swing this season. Saturday mornings bring fresh produce, handmade crafts, and food trucks to downtown. It is a great way to support local vendors while stocking up on weekend essentials.

For vintage treasures, Cooper Street Antique Mall houses over 200 booths in 53,000 square feet of shopping space. You could spend hours here and still not see everything.

Plan Your Weekend

Whether you are a longtime resident or just visiting, Arlington this weekend has something for everyone. From world-class entertainment to neighborhood gems, our city continues to prove why it is one of the most dynamic places in Texas.
