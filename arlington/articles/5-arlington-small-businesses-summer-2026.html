5 Arlington Small Businesses Making Waves This Summer

From craft cocktails to artisan coffee, these local gems are defining Arlington's vibrant scene

Published June 1, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Summer has officially arrived in The American Dream City, and Arlington's small business scene is hotter than ever. While the crowds flock to AT&T Stadium and Six Flags, savvy locals know that the real magic happens in the neighborhood shops, restaurants, and creative spaces that give Arlington its unique character.

This summer, a new wave of entrepreneurs and established favorites are raising the bar for what it means to do business in Arlington. From innovative dining concepts to community-focused retail, these five businesses represent the best of what makes our city special.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Craft Meets Community

If you haven't experienced The Tipsy Oak yet, you're missing out on one of downtown Arlington's crown jewels. This gastropub has mastered the art of combining elevated comfort food with an atmosphere that feels like your favorite neighborhood hangout.

Their outdoor patio is the place to be on summer evenings, offering a perfect view of downtown's vibrant energy while you sip on craft cocktails made with house-infused spirits. The menu rotates seasonally, but favorites like their signature burgers and truffle fries keep regulars coming back. With live music on weekends and a commitment to sourcing ingredients from Texas producers, The Tipsy Oak embodies everything great about Arlington's local business culture.

The Tipsy Oak

A downtown gastropub combining elevated comfort food with craft cocktails and a vibrant patio atmosphere. Perfect for summer evenings with live music and locally-sourced ingredients.

Division Brewing: Arlington's Craft Beer Pioneer

Located in the heart of the Urban Union District, Division Brewing has become the unofficial community center for Arlington's craft beer enthusiasts. What started as a passion project has grown into a destination brewery that draws visitors from across DFW.

Their taproom features rotating experimental brews alongside flagship favorites, with styles ranging from hazy IPAs to rich stouts and crisp lagers. The outdoor beer garden is perfect for summer afternoons, and their commitment to collaboration means you'll often find food trucks, pop-up vendors, and local artists sharing the space.

Division Brewing

A destination brewery in the Urban Union District featuring experimental and flagship craft beers. The outdoor beer garden hosts food trucks, local artists, and community events.

Hurtado Barbecue: Texas Smoke Done Right

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands in a league of its own. Their brisket has earned regional recognition for its perfect bark, tender texture, and deep smoky flavor that can only come from years of mastering the craft.

Located in downtown Arlington, Hurtado has become a pilgrimage site for BBQ lovers. The line forms early, and for good reason—their house-made sausages, fall-off-the-bone ribs, and legendary sides like elote creamed corn sell out fast. Owner Brandon Hurtado's dedication to traditional Central Texas barbecue techniques while incorporating bold flavors has created something truly special.

Hurtado Barbecue

Regionally acclaimed Texas barbecue in downtown Arlington. Famous for perfectly smoked brisket, house-made sausages, and legendary sides. A must-visit for BBQ enthusiasts.

Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters: Your Daily Ritual Elevated

For those who believe coffee is more than just caffeine, Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters delivers an experience that rivals anything in Dallas or Fort Worth. Their downtown location has become a gathering place for remote workers, students, and coffee connoisseurs alike.

The brothers behind the operation source beans directly from farmers around the world, roasting them in small batches to bring out unique flavor profiles. Whether you're ordering a perfectly pulled espresso or a refreshing cold brew on a hot summer day, the quality is unmistakable.

Salter Bros. Coffee Roasters

Artisan coffee roasters in downtown Arlington sourcing beans directly from farmers worldwide. Small-batch roasting creates unique flavor profiles in a warm, industrial-chic space.

Texas Live!: Entertainment District's Crown Jewel

No discussion of Arlington businesses would be complete without mentioning Texas Live! This entertainment complex has transformed the area around Globe Life Field and AT&T Stadium into a destination unto itself.

With multiple restaurants, bars, and entertainment venues under one roof, Texas Live! offers something for everyone. Watch the game on massive screens at the Sports Bar, enjoy live music at the outdoor plaza, or experience upscale dining at one of their signature restaurants.

Texas Live!

The ultimate entertainment complex near Globe Life Field and AT&T Stadium. Multiple restaurants, bars, live music venues, and massive screens for watching games.

Why Supporting Local Matters

These five businesses represent just a fraction of what makes Arlington special. When you spend your dollars at locally-owned establishments, you're not just getting great products and services—you're investing in your community. Local businesses create jobs, support other local suppliers, and give Arlington the unique character that sets it apart from generic chain-store suburbs.

This summer, make it a point to explore something new in Arlington. Try that restaurant you've been meaning to visit, grab coffee from a local roaster instead of a chain, or discover a shop you never knew existed. The American Dream City is built on the dreams of entrepreneurs who chose to make their mark here—and they need your support to keep thriving.

Get out there and discover why Arlington's small business scene is becoming the envy of the Metroplex. Your new favorite spot is waiting.
