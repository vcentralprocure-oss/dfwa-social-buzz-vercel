Arlington's End-of-Summer Guide: 5 Local Businesses to Visit Before Fall

From craft breweries to legendary bakeries, discover where to make the most of summer's final weeks in The American Dream City

Photo by Trac Vu on Unsplash

Summer is drawing to a close in The American Dream City, but there's still time to make memories at some of Arlington's best local spots. Whether you're looking for one last patio dinner, a craft beer to toast the season, or a sweet treat before the school year kicks into high gear, these five local businesses are serving up the best of Arlington's late summer vibes.

🍺 The Tipsy Oak

With its massive patio and rotating selection of craft beers, The Tipsy Oak is the perfect spot to soak up the final days of summer. Located in the heart of downtown Arlington, this gastropub has become a community gathering place where locals come for the burgers and stay for the atmosphere. Their outdoor seating area is ideal for those warm August evenings, and their craft cocktail menu features seasonal specials that capture the essence of summer.

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

No Texas summer is complete without barbecue, and Hurtado Barbecue delivers some of the best in the Metroplex. This fourth-generation barbecue joint has earned national recognition for its perfectly smoked brisket and house-made sausages. Located on Division Street, Hurtado's is the kind of place where you can taste the tradition in every bite. Don't miss their late summer specials before the season changes.

🍺 Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery, Division Brewing, continues to innovate with small-batch brews that reflect the character of our city. Their taproom on Main Street is a hub of local culture, featuring live music on weekends and a dog-friendly patio that's perfect for lazy summer afternoons. Grab a flight and discover why Division Brewing has become a cornerstone of Arlington's craft beer scene.

🥖 Marquez Bakery

For over 30 years, Marquez Bakery has been serving up authentic Mexican baked goods and tortillas that have become legendary in Arlington. This family-owned gem on Pioneer Parkway is worth the trip for their fresh flour tortillas alone—made daily using a traditional recipe that has remained unchanged for generations. Stock up on their famous tortillas and pan dulce before summer ends.

🍜 Rocketbelly

Texas' first self-serve boba bar, Rocketbelly, has quickly become a downtown Arlington favorite. Create your own custom drink with their innovative self-serve system, choosing from dozens of flavors and toppings. It's the perfect refreshing treat for those hot August days, and their Instagram-worthy aesthetic makes every visit a shareable experience.

Make the Most of Summer's Final Weeks

As we transition from summer to fall, these local businesses remind us why Arlington is such a special place to live, work, and play. Each one represents the entrepreneurial spirit and community focus that makes The American Dream City truly unique.

Support local, make memories, and enjoy everything Arlington has to offer before the seasons change!
