Your Weekend Guide to Arlington: Free Concerts, New Eats & Summer Vibes

July 16, 2026 | Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Yvette de Wit on Unsplash

Summer in The American Dream City is in full swing, and there's no shortage of ways to make the most of your weekend. From free outdoor concerts to exciting new restaurant openings, here's your curated guide to the best of Arlington this July.

Free Music Under the Stars at Levitt Pavilion

The Levitt Pavilion continues its beloved summer concert series, bringing free live music to downtown Arlington every weekend. Located at 100 W. Abram Street, this outdoor venue has become the heartbeat of our city's cultural scene.

With over 50 free concerts annually, the Levitt showcases everything from Texas country and Latin rhythms to indie rock and blues. Pack a blanket, grab some friends, and enjoy an evening of music under the Texas stars. The pavilion also hosts Family Movie Nights throughout the summer—perfect for creating memories with the kids.

New Flavors Hit Downtown Arlington

Arlington's culinary scene keeps getting better. This summer has welcomed some exciting newcomers that locals are already raving about.

Coquí Café

Brings a taste of Puerto Rico to South Collins Street. This vibrant spot serves up authentic island flavors alongside a mission to share food and culture with the Arlington community. From traditional pastries to bold coffee, it's quickly becoming a favorite morning stop.

Rocketbelly

Satisfying Arlington's boba cravings with creative tea drinks and sweet treats. Located in the heart of downtown, this trendy spot is perfect for cooling off during those scorching Texas afternoons.

Blue Mint Thai

Fresh, locally-owned Thai cuisine that rivals anything in the metroplex. Their pad thai and green curry are already generating buzz among food enthusiasts.

Established Favorites Worth Revisiting

While new spots are exciting, Arlington's established gems continue to deliver exceptional experiences.

The Tipsy Oak

Remains the go-to for craft cocktails and elevated pub fare. Their rooftop patio offers one of the best outdoor dining experiences in the city—perfect for summer evenings.

Hurtado Barbecue

Continues to draw crowds from across DFW with their award-winning brisket and unique Tex-Mex BBQ fusion. If you haven't tried their famous brisket tacos, you're missing out on a true Arlington original.

Plan Your Perfect Arlington Weekend

Start your Saturday with coffee at Coquí Café, spend the afternoon exploring downtown's shops, then head to Levitt Pavilion for an evening of free music. On Sunday, grab brunch at The Tipsy Oak followed by a relaxing boba from Rocketbelly.

That's the beauty of Arlington—world-class entertainment, diverse dining, and genuine community spirit, all within reach.
