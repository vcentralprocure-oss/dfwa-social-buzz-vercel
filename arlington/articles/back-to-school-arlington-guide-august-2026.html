Back to School in Arlington: Your Guide to the Final Days of Summer

August 3, 2026 · Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Tomek Baginski on Unsplash

The backpacks are packed, the school supplies are sorted, and Arlington ISD students are counting down their final days of freedom. With the first day of school set for Wednesday, August 12, 2026, families across The American Dream City are squeezing every last drop out of summer break.

But before the alarm clocks start ringing at 6 AM, there's still time to make memories. Whether you're looking for one last family adventure, a spot to grab breakfast before the morning rush, or a place to celebrate the end of summer, Arlington's local businesses have you covered.

Where to Fuel Up for the School Year

Before the chaos of carpool lines and homework begins, treat your family to a meal at one of these Arlington favorites:

Division Street Diner

Start your day the right way at this Arlington breakfast institution. Known for their Country Eggs Benedict and legendary Blueberry Pancakes, Division Street Diner is the perfect spot for that "last weekend of summer" family breakfast. Their lunch menu of burgers, salads, and sandwiches will fuel you up for back-to-school shopping.

The Tipsy Oak

This gastropub and icehouse on East Front Street has become a downtown staple for families and friends alike. With chef-driven menus, craft beer, and a welcoming patio, it's the ideal spot for parents to toast the end of summer (and the beginning of school-year routines).

Hurtado Barbecue

The official barbecue restaurant of the Texas Rangers brings award-winning Tex-Mex BBQ to downtown Arlington. Their championship-worthy brisket and unique flavor combinations make it a must-visit before the busy school year kicks into high gear.

Rocketbelly

Texas' first and only self-serve boba tea bar is a hit with kids and teens. This space-themed spot features Rocket Chicken, custom bubble teas, and plenty of games and activities—perfect for one last summer treat before homework takes over.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery offers unique craft flavors for the adults, from refreshing blondes to hoppy IPAs. Their laid-back atmosphere is perfect for decompressing after a long day of back-to-school preparation.

Back-to-School Events You Won't Want to Miss

The Arlington ISD Back to School Kickoff just wrapped up at AT&T Stadium on August 1st, drawing thousands of families for free supplies, health screenings, and community resources. If you missed it, don't worry—many local organizations are still hosting supply drives and readiness events throughout early August.

Keep an eye on the Levitt Pavilion schedule too. Their free outdoor concert series continues through the summer, offering the perfect low-cost family entertainment before homework and extracurriculars take over your evenings.

Make the Most of These Final Days

Whether you're a parent ready for routine or a student clinging to every last moment of freedom, these final days of summer are precious. Arlington's local businesses are here to help you make them count—from morning pancakes to evening patio sessions.

The school bell rings on August 12th. Until then, get out there and enjoy everything The American Dream City has to offer.
