Your Ultimate Guide to Arlington's Entertainment District This Summer

Published May 20, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Summer is here, and there's no better place to be than Arlington's Entertainment District. Whether you're a local looking for weekend plans or visiting for the first time, this vibrant neighborhood offers something for everyone—from world-class sports and live music to incredible dining and nightlife.

Photo by Max Fray on Unsplash

Where the Action Lives

The Entertainment District is home to some of Texas's most iconic venues. Globe Life Field, the stunning home of the 2023 World Series champion Texas Rangers, hosts baseball games, concerts, and special events year-round. Just steps away, AT&T Stadium—nicknamed "Jerry World"—continues to draw massive crowds for Dallas Cowboys games, concerts, and major events.

But the district isn't just about sports. Texas Live! has transformed the area into a true entertainment destination. This sprawling complex combines live music venues, restaurants, bars, and outdoor spaces where you can catch everything from local bands to national touring acts.

Eat, Drink & Be Merry

All that entertainment works up an appetite, and the district delivers with an impressive food scene. Here are five spots you need to check out:

Babe's Chicken Dinner House — This Arlington institution has been serving up legendary fried chicken, chicken-fried steak, and all the fixings since 2010. The family-style dining and down-home atmosphere make it perfect for groups.

Pony Up — Located right in the heart of Texas Live!, this upscale sports bar offers craft cocktails, an extensive beer selection, and elevated bar food with views of the action.

Lockhart Smokehouse — When you're craving authentic Texas barbecue, this is the spot. Their brisket and sausage are smoked low and slow, just like they do it in the BBQ capital of Texas.

Tavern — A local favorite with locations throughout DFW, their Arlington outpost serves up great burgers, cold beer, and a laid-back vibe perfect for pre- or post-game gatherings.

Hooligan's Pub — Located in nearby downtown Arlington, this craft beer haven offers an ever-rotating selection of local and regional brews, plus a welcoming atmosphere that keeps regulars coming back.

Summer Events You Can't Miss

The Entertainment District really comes alive during summer. From outdoor concerts at Texas Live! to special promotions at Globe Life Field, there's always something happening. Check the City of Arlington's community calendar for free family-friendly events throughout the season.

Plan Your Visit

The Entertainment District is easily accessible from I-30 and Highway 360. Parking is plentiful, though it can fill up quickly on game days—consider arriving early or using rideshare options. Many venues offer special packages that combine dining, entertainment, and event tickets, so check their websites before you go.

Whether you're catching a Rangers game, dancing to live music, or simply enjoying a cold drink on a patio, Arlington's Entertainment District delivers the kind of summer memories that keep you coming back.
