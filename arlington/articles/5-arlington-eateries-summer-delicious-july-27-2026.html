5 Arlington Eateries Keeping Summer Delicious

From craft BBQ to tropical tiki drinks, these local spots are serving up the best flavors of the season

July 27, 2026 • 5 min read

Photo by Nguyễn Hiệp on Unsplash

Summer in Arlington isn't just about surviving the heat—it's about finding the perfect spots to enjoy it. While temperatures climb into the triple digits, local restaurateurs and brewers have created oases where you can beat the heat while discovering why The American Dream City has become a serious food destination.

The Tipsy Oak has mastered the art of the patio experience. Located in the heart of North Arlington, this gastropub combines craft cocktails with a rotating menu that celebrates Texas flavors. Their outdoor seating area, complete with misters and fans, transforms even the hottest afternoons into comfortable dining experiences. The bar program here deserves special mention—mixologists create seasonal drinks that incorporate local ingredients and unexpected flavor combinations. Visit The Tipsy Oak for happy hour specials that run late into the evening.

For barbecue enthusiasts, Hurtado Barbecue represents everything great about Texas smoking traditions. What started as a passion project has evolved into one of Arlington's most celebrated restaurants. The brisket here achieves that perfect balance of bark and tenderness that pitmasters spend years perfecting. Their downtown location has become a gathering place where families, college students, and office workers unite over plates of smoked meat. The outdoor seating area captures the casual, communal spirit that makes Texas barbecue culture so special. Check out Hurtado Barbecue for their daily specials.

Division Brewing stands as a testament to Arlington's growing craft beer scene. This independently owned brewery has built a reputation for experimental brews that push boundaries while remaining approachable. Their taproom, housed in a converted industrial space, offers a laid-back atmosphere where beer lovers can sample everything from crisp lagers to bold imperial stouts. The outdoor beer garden hosts food trucks and live music, creating a community hub that feels distinctly Arlington. Explore Division Brewing and their rotating tap list.

When you're craving something tropical, 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge transports you straight to the islands without leaving the metroplex. This South Arlington gem specializes in elaborate tiki cocktails served in creative vessels that make every visit feel like a special occasion. The dimly lit interior provides welcome relief from the summer sun, while the menu of Polynesian-inspired dishes complements the extensive rum selection perfectly. Discover 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge for their signature drinks.

Finally, Rocketbelly brings something completely different to the Arlington dining landscape. As Texas' first self-serve boba bar, this innovative spot lets customers customize their drinks with an array of teas, flavors, and toppings. The bright, modern space appeals to younger crowds while offering enough variety to satisfy any palate. It's the perfect afternoon pick-me-up when you need something refreshing and unique. Try Rocketbelly for a custom boba experience.

These five businesses represent just a slice of what makes Arlington's food scene so exciting right now. Each brings something unique to the table while contributing to the city's growing reputation as a destination for food lovers. Whether you're a longtime resident or just passing through, make time to explore these local gems.
