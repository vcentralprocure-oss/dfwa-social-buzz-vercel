Your Ultimate Guide to July 4th Weekend in Arlington

From Firecracker 5K to spectacular fireworks at Levitt Pavilion — everything you need to celebrate Independence Day in The American Dream City

June 26, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Summer is in full swing, and Arlington is ready to celebrate Independence Day in style. From early morning races to spectacular fireworks shows, here's everything you need to know to make the most of your July 4th weekend in The American Dream City.

Start Your Day with the Firecracker 5K

The festivities kick off bright and early at 7 a.m. with the third annual Firecracker 5K at Levitt Pavilion. This family-friendly race follows the parade route through Downtown Arlington, offering two laps of scenic city views before finishing back at the pavilion. Whether you're a serious runner or just looking for a fun way to start the holiday, this event welcomes participants of all ages and fitness levels.

Light Up Arlington: The Main Event

As the sun sets, Downtown Arlington transforms into a patriotic paradise. Light Up Arlington is the city's signature summer celebration, drawing thousands of locals and visitors alike. The Levitt Pavilion will feature live music from Chef Dee at 7 p.m. and Brad Thompson at 8:30 p.m., setting the perfect soundtrack for your evening.

Food trucks will line the streets, offering everything from classic American fare to international cuisine. Bring a blanket, grab a spot on the lawn, and enjoy one of the best local fireworks shows in the entire Dallas-Fort Worth area.

Local Businesses Making the Celebration Special

No Arlington celebration is complete without supporting the local businesses that make our community thrive. Here are five spots to check out this weekend:

Hurtado Barbecue – This award-winning BBQ joint in Downtown Arlington has become a Texas phenomenon. Known for their famous "Texas Twinkies" and mouthwatering brisket, Brandon and Hannah Hurtado have created something truly special. Their Hatch chili mac & cheese is an instant fan favorite, and the Tres Leches Banana Pudding is the perfect sweet ending to any meal.

Division Brewing – Arlington's first craft brewery offers a rotating selection of house-made beers in a laid-back atmosphere. Their outdoor patio is the perfect spot to cool off with a cold one after the fireworks.

Levitt Pavilion – More than just a concert venue, this outdoor amphitheater hosts over 50 free concerts annually. Their July 4th programming is always a highlight of the summer season.

Arlington Convention & Visitors Bureau – Your go-to resource for everything happening in Arlington, from major events to hidden local gems.

Downtown Arlington – The heart of our city's cultural scene, featuring local shops, restaurants, and year-round events that bring the community together.

Plan Your Weekend

The July 4th celebrations are just the beginning. Arlington's summer calendar is packed with events, from outdoor concerts at Levitt Pavilion to family activities at River Legacy Parks. Mark your calendar for First Thursdays in Downtown Arlington, featuring free dance classes, food specials, and that famous foam party bus the kids love.

Whether you're a longtime resident or visiting for the weekend, Arlington knows how to celebrate summer right. Grab your red, white, and blue, and we'll see you downtown!
