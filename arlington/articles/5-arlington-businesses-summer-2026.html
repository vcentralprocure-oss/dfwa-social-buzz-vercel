5 Arlington Businesses You Need to Know About This Summer

From craft cocktails to artisan coffee, these local spots are defining Arlington's vibrant scene

May 21, 2026 · By Arlington Pulse

Photo by Trac Vu on Unsplash

Summer is officially here in Arlington, and there's never been a better time to explore what our local business community has to offer. While the big attractions like Six Flags and Globe Life Field get most of the attention, it's the neighborhood gems that truly make this city special.

Where the Locals Go for Great Food

If you're looking for authentic Texas barbecue with a side of Arlington pride, Smoke Sessions Barbecue should be at the top of your list. Located just off Collins Street, this family-owned joint has been serving up brisket that rivals anything you'll find in Austin. Their secret? A 16-hour smoke process using post oak wood sourced from Central Texas. The result is meat so tender it practically melts on your fork.

Smoke Sessions Barbecue

Authentic Central Texas-style barbecue with 16-hour smoked brisket and post oak wood. A local favorite off Collins Street.

Visit Website →

Speaking of local favorites, Babe's Chicken Dinner House in the Entertainment District continues to draw crowds with their legendary family-style dining. The fried chicken here isn't just a meal—it's an experience. Crispy, golden, and seasoned with a secret blend of spices, it's the kind of dish that keeps locals coming back decade after decade.

Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Legendary family-style dining in the Entertainment District. Famous for crispy, golden fried chicken and Southern hospitality.

Visit Website →

Craft Coffee and Community Vibes

Arlington's coffee scene has exploded in recent years, and White Rhino Coffee is leading the charge. With multiple locations across the city, including their flagship spot in Downtown Arlington, White Rhino has become the unofficial meeting place for entrepreneurs, students, and remote workers. Their house-made syrups and commitment to ethically sourced beans set them apart from the chain competition.

White Rhino Coffee

Downtown Arlington's favorite coffee spot. House-made syrups, ethically sourced beans, and a community-focused atmosphere with open mic nights.

Visit Website →

The Downtown location deserves special mention for its community-focused approach. They host open mic nights, local art displays, and even offer a "pay it forward" board where customers can buy drinks for strangers. It's the kind of place where baristas remember your name and your order.

Entertainment District Hotspots

No discussion of Arlington businesses would be complete without mentioning Texas Live! This sprawling entertainment complex has transformed the area around Globe Life Field into a destination unto itself. With multiple restaurants, bars, and live music venues under one roof, it's the perfect spot for pre-game gatherings or weekend celebrations.

Texas Live!

The ultimate entertainment destination near Globe Life Field. Multiple restaurants, bars, live music venues, and pre-game celebrations.

Visit Website →

The Pony Up rooftop bar offers stunning views of the stadiums along with craft cocktails that change with the seasons. Their summer menu features locally-inspired drinks like the "Rangers Refresher"—a grapefruit and vodka concoction that's become an instant hit with baseball fans.

Pony Up

Rooftop bar at Texas Live! with stunning stadium views and seasonal craft cocktails. Try the famous "Rangers Refresher."

Visit Website →

Why Supporting Local Matters

When you spend money at local Arlington businesses, you're doing more than just getting a great meal or a perfect cup of coffee. You're supporting your neighbors, helping create jobs, and keeping our community unique. These businesses give Arlington its character—they're what makes this city feel like home.

So this summer, skip the chains and discover what makes Arlington's local business scene so special. Whether you're a lifelong resident or just passing through, these spots offer a taste of what makes our city truly one-of-a-kind.
