River Legacy Fall Festival Returns to Arlington October 10

Family fall activities, nature experiences and practical details for this Saturday’s Arlington event

Looking for a fall outing that feels close to home? River Legacy Foundation’s annual Fall Festival returns to Arlington on Saturday, October 10, bringing a full day of outdoor activities, seasonal fun and nature-focused learning to River Legacy Park and the River Legacy Nature Center.

The festival runs from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. at 701 NW Green Oaks Boulevard. Individual tickets are listed at $8 for ages 3 and older, making it a relatively accessible option for families planning a weekend activity together.

What can visitors expect? Organizers have highlighted guided nature hikes, live animal presentations, hands-on crafts, a pumpkin patch, games, food trucks, live music and outdoor challenges. The mix gives families a reason to spend time outside while also introducing kids to the wildlife and habitats found around Arlington.

The festival also marks a milestone for the Nature Center, which is celebrating its 30th anniversary in 2026. The center is part of a public-private partnership supporting environmental education and the preservation of the surrounding parkland.

Make a plan before you go. The event is scheduled across a seven-hour window, so families may want to arrive earlier for activities they especially want to catch and leave room for breaks. Food vendors are expected, but checking the event organizer’s latest details before leaving home is a smart move for any schedule changes.

One important note for pet owners: pets are not permitted at the festival; trained service animals are welcome. Parking is available in the area, though spaces may be limited, so allow extra time for arrival.

Why it’s worth a look: River Legacy Park is already one of north Arlington’s major outdoor spaces, with wooded areas, wetlands and prairie habitat. The Fall Festival adds a special event atmosphere to that natural setting, pairing family entertainment with a reminder of the local environment the park helps protect.

Before heading out, confirm ticket availability and any final event updates with the organizer. If you’re looking for a seasonal outing that combines fresh air, hands-on activities and a distinctly Arlington setting, this Saturday’s festival is one to put on the calendar.
