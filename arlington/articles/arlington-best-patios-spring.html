Arlington's Best Patios: Where to Eat Outside This Spring

From downtown hideaways to Entertainment District gems, these outdoor dining spots are perfect for soaking up the Texas sunshine

May 9, 2026

Photo by Fallon Michael on Unsplash

Spring has officially arrived in Arlington, and there's no better way to enjoy it than with a meal on one of the city's best patios. Whether you're looking for a casual spot near UTA, a family-friendly venue in the Entertainment District, or a romantic dinner under the stars, Arlington's outdoor dining scene has something for everyone.

We've rounded up five local favorites where the food is great and the outdoor vibes are even better. These aren't chain restaurants with cookie-cutter setups—these are Arlington institutions that have perfected the art of al fresco dining.

The Grease Monkey: A Downtown Classic

If you haven't experienced The Grease Monkey yet, you're missing out on one of downtown Arlington's most beloved spots. This burger joint has been serving up some of the best patties in DFW for years, and their patio is the perfect place to enjoy them. With its laid-back atmosphere and prime people-watching location, it's a favorite among UTA students and locals alike. The outdoor seating fills up fast on weekends, so arrive early or be prepared to wait—but trust us, it's worth it.

Visit The Grease Monkey

Babe's Chicken Dinner House: Family-Style Feasting

For a true Texas experience, head to Babe's Chicken Dinner House in the Arlington Highlands. This legendary spot serves fried chicken and comfort sides family-style, and their spacious patio is ideal for groups. The rustic outdoor seating area captures that classic Texas charm, complete with string lights that make evening dinners feel magical. It's the kind of place where you can relax, unwind, and let someone else do the cooking for a change.

Visit Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Division Brewing: Craft Beer & Open Air

Arlington's craft beer scene has exploded in recent years, and Division Brewing leads the pack. Located in the heart of downtown, this local brewery features a fantastic outdoor patio where you can sip house-made brews under the Texas sky. They frequently host food trucks, live music, and community events, making it more than just a place to grab a drink—it's a gathering spot for the entire community. Bring your dog, bring your friends, and settle in for an afternoon of great beer and good times.

Visit Division Brewing

El Gabacho Tex-Mex Smokehouse: Where BBQ Meets Patio Perfection

Tex-Mex and barbecue might seem like an unusual combination, but El Gabacho Tex-Mex Smokehouse makes it work beautifully. Their outdoor seating area is spacious and inviting, with plenty of shade for those hot Texas afternoons. The menu features smoked meats with a Mexican twist, and the margaritas are not to be missed. It's become a go-to spot for families and friend groups looking to enjoy a leisurely meal without leaving the city.

Visit El Gabacho Tex-Mex Smokehouse

Old School Pizza Tavern: Slice of Heaven Outdoors

Sometimes you just want a great slice of pizza and a cold drink, and Old School Pizza Tavern delivers exactly that. Their patio is one of the most underrated outdoor dining spots in Arlington—comfortable, casual, and perfect for everything from date nights to post-game celebrations. With a wide selection of craft beers on tap and pizzas that rival anything in Dallas or Fort Worth, it's no wonder locals keep coming back.

Visit Old School Pizza Tavern

Why Outdoor Dining Matters for Arlington

Supporting these local restaurants does more than just fill your stomach—it strengthens our community. Every dollar spent at an Arlington-owned business circulates back into our local economy, supporting jobs, funding community initiatives, and helping our city thrive. Plus, dining outdoors lets you experience Arlington's beautiful spring weather while connecting with neighbors and friends.

So grab your sunglasses, pick a patio, and make the most of this perfect patio season. Your taste buds—and your community—will thank you.
