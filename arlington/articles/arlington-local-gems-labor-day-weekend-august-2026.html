5 Arlington Local Gems You Need to Visit This Labor Day Weekend

From award-winning BBQ to craft brews, discover the local businesses making The American Dream City shine this holiday weekend

Photo by MJ Tangonan on Unsplash

As summer winds down and Labor Day weekend approaches, there's no better time to explore what makes Arlington special. While everyone else is fighting traffic to get out of town, savvy locals know the real treasures are right here in our own backyard.

From Michelin-listed barbecue to Arlington's original craft brewery, these five local gems are where the real magic happens. No chains, no franchises, just pure Arlington soul.

Hurtado Barbecue

When Texas Monthly names you one of the best barbecue joints in the state, you know you're doing something right. Hurtado Barbecue has become a destination for meat lovers across DFW, serving Central Texas-style barbecue with a Tex-Mex twist. Their brisket is the stuff of legend, but don't sleep on the birria tacos or brisket tostadas. Located at 205 E Front St in the heart of downtown.

The Tipsy Oak

Just steps from Hurtado, The Tipsy Oak is the kind of neighborhood gastropub that makes you feel like a regular on your first visit. With a chef-driven menu, craft beer selection, and that unbeatable downtown patio, it's the perfect spot to unwind after exploring Arlington. Live music on weekends adds to the vibe.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first and only locally-owned production brewery, Division Brewing has been pouring award-winning craft beer since 2016. Their taproom on Main Street is where beer enthusiasts gather to sample everything from hazy IPAs to rich stouts. The dog-friendly patio and rotating food trucks make it a weekend staple.

Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

A fourth-generation family business, Marquez Bakery has been serving Arlington since 1962. Their handmade flour tortillas are widely considered the best in Texas, and the bakery case is filled with authentic Mexican pastries that will transport you straight to abuela's kitchen. A true taste of Arlington history.

Daydream Bookstore Cafe

The newest addition to downtown, Daydream combines a romance-only bookstore with a cozy coffee shop for the ultimate cozy experience. Located at 380 E Front St, it's the perfect spot to discover your next read while sipping a latte. Like the setting of a Hallmark movie, but real and right here in Arlington.

Why Local Matters

When you spend your dollars at local businesses, you're not just getting better food, drinks, and experiences. You're supporting Arlington families, keeping money in our community, and helping The American Dream City live up to its name.

This Labor Day weekend, skip the crowds and discover why locals love Arlington. These five spots are just the beginning.
