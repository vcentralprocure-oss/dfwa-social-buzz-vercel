This Weekend in Arlington: Live Music, World Cup Watch Parties & Summer Fun

June 20, 2026 • Arlington Pulse

Photo by Trac Vu on Unsplash

Happy Saturday, Arlington! The summer heat is on, but that hasn't stopped The American Dream City from delivering an incredible weekend lineup. Whether you're a soccer fanatic ready to cheer on Team USA in the FIFA World Cup 2026 or you're looking for the perfect patio to beat the heat, we've got you covered with everything happening June 20-21, 2026.

⚽ World Cup Fever Hits Arlington

Arlington is officially in the global spotlight as FIFA World Cup 2026 matches continue at AT&T Stadium. This weekend, soccer fans from around the world are converging on our city to experience the beautiful game at one of the world's most impressive venues.

Not heading to the stadium? No problem! Texas Live! is hosting massive watch parties on their 100-foot screen — the ultimate way to experience the matches with fellow fans. Plus, they're giving away official country jerseys during select matches. Just scan the QR code on screen, join their VIP Club, and you're entered to win.

🎵 Free Live Music at Levitt Pavilion

Looking for something that won't cost a dime? The Levitt Pavilion continues its free summer concert series this weekend. Pack a blanket, grab some takeout from a local spot, and enjoy live music under the stars in downtown Arlington. It's the perfect way to spend a summer evening without breaking the bank.

🍽️ Where to Eat This Weekend

All that cheering and dancing works up an appetite. Here are five local Arlington businesses you need to check out this weekend:

The Tipsy Oak

This downtown favorite combines craft cocktails with elevated bar food in a space that feels like your coolest friend's backyard. Their patio is the perfect spot to cool off with one of their signature frozen drinks. Weekend brunch is also a must-try!

Visit Website →

Hurtado Barbecue

Texas Monthly called them one of the best new BBQ joints in the state, and for good reason. Their brisket is the stuff of legends, and the house-made sausages will ruin you for grocery store links forever. Get there early — when they sell out, they close!

Visit Website →

Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery is still one of its best. With a rotating tap list that ranges from crushable lagers to experimental sours, there's something for every beer lover. Their outdoor seating area is dog-friendly, so bring your pup!

Visit Website →

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge

Escape to a tropical paradise without leaving Arlington. This tiki-themed bar serves up some of the most creative (and potent) cocktails in the city. The kitschy decor and island vibes make it feel like a mini-vacation from the Texas heat.

Visit Website →

Nehemiah Coffee Co.

Need a caffeine fix before your weekend adventures? This local roaster is serving up some of the best coffee in DFW. Their beans are ethically sourced and roasted in small batches, and the baristas actually know what they're doing. Try their cold brew — it's liquid gold on a hot day.

Visit Website →

🌟 New Business Welcome Kits

The City of Arlington recently launched a new Business Welcome Kit program for local eateries, funded by a community grant. This initiative helps new restaurants get connected with local resources and customers. It's just one more way Arlington is supporting its small business community.

🏗️ Downtown's Urban Union District Expands

The much-anticipated expansion of Downtown Arlington's Urban Union District is nearing completion. This latest development will introduce new retail shops, expanded restaurant offerings, lifestyle services, and even a boutique hotel. Keep your eyes peeled for new openings in the coming weeks!
