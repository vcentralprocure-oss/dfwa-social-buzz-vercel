This Weekend in Arlington: Free Concerts, Farmers Market & Summer Fun

Your complete guide to June 6-7, 2026 — from Levitt Pavilion live music to downtown's best local spots

Published June 5, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Free live music returns to Levitt Pavilion this Saturday night

Summer is in full swing in Arlington, Texas, and this weekend promises something for everyone. Whether you're craving live music under the stars, farm-fresh produce, or a cold craft beer on a sunny patio, The American Dream City has you covered.

Free Live Music at Levitt Pavilion

Saturday night brings The Branded Saddle Boys to the Levitt Pavilion stage at 7:30 PM. This Texas-based rock n' roll band delivers an energetic performance that captures the spirit of the Lone Star State. The best part? It's completely free.

The Levitt Pavilion has become the heart of downtown Arlington's cultural scene, hosting more than 50 free outdoor concerts each year. Bring a blanket, pack a picnic, and enjoy an evening of live music under the Texas sky. The venue opens at 6:00 PM, so arrive early to snag a good spot on the lawn.

Downtown Farmers Market

Saturday morning is perfect for a stroll through the Downtown Arlington Farmers Market. Running from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, this weekly market features local farmers, artisans, and food vendors offering everything from fresh produce to handmade crafts.

It's more than just shopping — it's a community gathering. Meet the people growing your food, discover unique local products, and support Arlington's small business ecosystem. The market is located in the heart of downtown, making it easy to combine with brunch or coffee at one of the nearby cafés.

Local Spots to Check Out

After the market or before the concert, make time to explore some of Arlington's standout local businesses. Here are five worth visiting this weekend:

Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery serves up unique craft beers in a laid-back atmosphere. Their downtown location at 506 E. Main St. features a rotating tap list and a welcoming patio perfect for summer afternoons.

Hurtado Barbecue

What started as pop-up events has exploded into one of Texas' most talked-about barbecue joints. Brandon Hurtado's smoked meats have earned a devoted following, and the downtown Arlington location remains the original — and many say the best.

The Tipsy Oak

This chef-driven gastropub on East Front Street combines craft beer, creative cocktails, and a menu that goes far beyond typical bar food. Their outdoor seating area is ideal for people-watching downtown.

Daydream Bookstore & Cafe

Arlington's newest literary hotspot specializes in romance novels and serves excellent coffee. Located at 380 E. Front St., it's a cozy retreat for book lovers and coffee enthusiasts alike.

Levitt Pavilion

Beyond this weekend's concert, the pavilion hosts free performances throughout the summer. Check their calendar for upcoming shows ranging from country and blues to Latin rhythms and indie rock.

Plan Your Weekend

Start Saturday morning at the farmers market, grab lunch at one of downtown's local restaurants, and cap off the evening with free live music at Levitt Pavilion. Sunday is perfect for a relaxed brunch and exploring Arlington's growing coffee scene.

The beauty of a weekend in Arlington is the variety. World-class entertainment venues sit alongside intimate local businesses. Big-city amenities meet small-town charm. And this weekend, it's all waiting for you.
