Your Weekend Guide to Arlington: Where to Eat, Drink & Explore

July 24, 2026 • Arlington Pulse • 5 min read

Photo by Justin Wolff on Unsplash

Looking for something to do this weekend in Arlington? Whether you're a longtime resident or just passing through The American Dream City, we've rounded up the best local spots to eat, drink, and make the most of your weekend.

From award-winning barbecue to craft breweries and rooftop patios, Arlington's local business scene is buzzing with energy. Here's your guide to five must-visit spots that capture the heart of our community.

Start Your Day Right

Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery is the perfect spot to kick off your weekend. Located in the heart of downtown, Division Brewing offers a rotating selection of house-made beers in a laid-back, community-focused atmosphere. Their outdoor patio is ideal for Texas summer evenings, and they regularly host live music and food trucks.

Visit Division Brewing →

Where the Locals Eat

Hurtado Barbecue

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue is in a league of its own. This local favorite has earned national recognition for their perfectly smoked brisket, creative sides, and welcoming atmosphere. The line might be long, but trust us—it's worth every minute. Pro tip: get there early before they sell out.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

The Tipsy Oak

For a more upscale dining experience without the stuffy atmosphere, The Tipsy Oak delivers. Their innovative menu features locally-sourced ingredients, craft cocktails, and one of the best patios in Arlington. Whether you're meeting friends for happy hour or enjoying a date night, this spot never disappoints.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

Hidden Gems Worth Finding

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge

Escape to a tropical paradise right here in Arlington. 4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge serves up expertly crafted tiki cocktails in an atmosphere that transports you straight to the islands. Their dedication to the craft of tropical drinks has made them a beloved local institution.

Visit 4 Kahunas →

Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

No Arlington food tour is complete without a stop at Marquez Bakery. This family-owned gem has been serving up fresh tortillas, authentic Mexican pastries, and homemade tamales for generations. Grab a dozen tortillas to go—you'll thank us later.

Visit Marquez Bakery →

Plan Your Weekend

Arlington's local business scene is thriving, and these five spots are just the beginning. Whether you're craving craft beer, world-class barbecue, or authentic Mexican flavors, our city delivers.

Support local this weekend and discover why Arlington is truly The American Dream City.
