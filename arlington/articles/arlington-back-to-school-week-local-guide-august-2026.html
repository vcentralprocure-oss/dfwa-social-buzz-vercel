Arlington's Back-to-School Week: 5 Local Spots to Start Your Year Right

From coffee shops to fuel your mornings to family-friendly restaurants for busy weeknights, discover the Arlington businesses making back-to-school season smoother

Photo by Unsplash

It's that time of year again—Arlington ISD students are heading back to the classroom, and families across The American Dream City are adjusting to new routines. Whether you're a parent juggling drop-offs and pickups, a college student at UTA starting a new semester, or a teacher preparing for the year ahead, one thing's for certain: you need fuel, convenience, and places that understand the back-to-school hustle.

We've rounded up five local Arlington businesses that are ready to make your transition back to school a little easier—and a lot more delicious.

☕ Nehemiah Coffee Co.

Early mornings require serious coffee, and Nehemiah Coffee Co. delivers some of the best specialty coffee in Arlington. Located near the UTA campus, this local roaster has built a reputation for ethically sourced beans and expertly crafted drinks. Their cozy atmosphere makes it the perfect spot for students to study or for parents to grab a quick pick-me-up before the morning rush. Don't miss their seasonal fall drinks launching later this month.

🥯 Marquez Bakery

For over three decades, Marquez Bakery on Pioneer Parkway has been an Arlington institution. Their fresh flour tortillas—made daily using a family recipe passed down through generations—are the perfect foundation for quick weeknight dinners. Grab a dozen tortillas, some pan dulce for the kids' breakfast, and their famous salsa to keep your family fed during those hectic first weeks of school.

🍔 The Tipsy Oak

When you need a break from cooking but want something better than fast food, The Tipsy Oak in downtown Arlington offers family-friendly dining without sacrificing quality. Their kids' menu keeps the little ones happy, while parents can enjoy craft beers and gourmet burgers. The laid-back atmosphere makes it ideal for those "I don't feel like cooking tonight" evenings that every family knows well.

📚 Half Price Books

Back-to-school shopping isn't complete without books, and Half Price Books on South Cooper Street is a treasure trove for students of all ages. From textbooks and study guides to bedtime stories for younger kids, this Arlington staple helps stretch your back-to-school budget. Their buy-back program also means you can trade in last year's books for store credit toward this year's needs.

🍜 Rocketbelly

Texas' first self-serve boba bar, Rocketbelly in downtown Arlington is the perfect after-school treat. Kids love creating their own custom drinks with the interactive self-serve system, and parents appreciate the quality ingredients and reasonable prices. It's become a popular spot for celebrating that first week back or simply cooling off after a long day of classes.

Start Your Year Strong

Back-to-school season doesn't have to be all stress and chaos. These five Arlington businesses understand what local families need—and they're ready to help you start the school year on the right foot. From morning coffee runs to after-school treats, The American Dream City has everything you need to make this your best year yet.
