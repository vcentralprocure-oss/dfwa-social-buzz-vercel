Your Mid-July Guide to Arlington: Free Concerts, Local Eats & Community Vibes

Published July 12, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Photo by Megan Bucknall on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in Arlington, and there's no shortage of ways to enjoy these long, hot days. Whether you're looking for free outdoor entertainment, craving a taste of something new, or just want to explore what makes our city special, we've got you covered.

Free Music Under the Stars

The Levitt Pavilion continues its beloved summer concert series this week, offering free outdoor performances right in the heart of downtown. Grab a blanket, pack some snacks, and settle in for an evening of live music under the Texas sky. The pavilion has become a true community gathering spot where neighbors meet, friends reconnect, and families make memories.

With more than 50 free concerts each year featuring everything from Texas country to Latin rhythms and indie sounds, there's always something new to discover. Check their schedule at levittpavilionarlington.org to see who's playing this week.

Local Businesses Worth Your Support

While you're out enjoying the city, make sure to stop by some of these local favorites that keep Arlington's economy vibrant and our community unique:

Coquí Café

This newly opened spot at 2442 S. Collins St. brings a taste of Puerto Rico to Arlington. More than just great coffee and authentic cuisine, Coquí Café represents the kind of entrepreneurial spirit that makes our city thrive. It's a perfect example of how small businesses enrich our cultural landscape while creating local jobs.

The Tipsy Oak

Located in the Urban Union District, this gastropub has become a downtown staple. With its craft cocktail program, rotating beer selection, and elevated comfort food, it's the ideal spot to cool off after exploring the city. Their patio seating offers a perfect vantage point for people-watching while enjoying Arlington's summer evenings.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery continues to draw crowds with its innovative small-batch beers and laid-back atmosphere. Located in the heart of downtown, it's become a community hub where locals gather to unwind and connect. Their taproom regularly hosts events that bring together beer enthusiasts and casual visitors alike.

Hurtado Barbecue

What started as a food truck has become one of the most celebrated barbecue spots in DFW. Their brisket has earned regional acclaim, but it's their commitment to the Arlington community that really sets them apart. Stop by for lunch and understand why locals have made this a regular destination.

Nehemiah Coffee Co.

This community-focused coffee shop does more than serve excellent brews. With a mission to create positive change in Arlington, Nehemiah has become a gathering place for everyone from students to professionals. Their downtown location makes it a convenient stop during your weekend explorations.

Why This Matters

Supporting local businesses isn't just about getting great food and drinks—it's about keeping Arlington's unique character alive. Every dollar spent at a local establishment circulates through our community, supporting jobs, funding local initiatives, and helping entrepreneurs pursue their dreams.

When you choose to grab coffee at a local café instead of a chain, or pick up dinner from a family-owned restaurant, you're voting for the kind of city you want to live in. You're helping ensure that Arlington remains vibrant, diverse, and full of opportunity.

This Week's Plan

Here's a simple way to make the most of mid-July in Arlington:

Start your weekend with coffee at Nehemiah Coffee Co. Then explore downtown and grab lunch at Hurtado Barbecue. Spend your evening at the Levitt Pavilion enjoying free live music. On Sunday, recover with brunch at The Tipsy Oak before checking out Division Brewing for an afternoon pint.

However you choose to spend your week, remember that Arlington's best experiences often come from the local businesses and community spaces that make our city special. Get out there, explore, and enjoy everything The American Dream City has to offer.
