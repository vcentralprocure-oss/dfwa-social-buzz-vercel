Your Ultimate Guide to This Week in Arlington: Free Concerts, Local Eats & Summer Vibes

From Levitt Pavilion's free music series to the best local patios, here's everything happening in The American Dream City June 14-20, 2026

Arlington Pulse • June 14, 2026

Photo by Yvette de Wit on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in Arlington, and this week is packed with can't-miss events, delicious dining spots, and plenty of reasons to get out and explore The American Dream City.

Whether you're a longtime local or just visiting for the FIFA World Cup festivities, we've rounded up the best of what's happening in Arlington from June 14-20, 2026.

Free Live Music at Levitt Pavilion

The heart of downtown Arlington comes alive every week with the Levitt Pavilion's free concert series. This week's lineup features everything from local singer-songwriters to tribute bands that'll have you dancing under the stars.

Grab a blanket, pack some snacks, and enjoy live music in one of the most beautiful outdoor venues in DFW. The best part? It won't cost you a dime.

Where to Eat This Week

Arlington's food scene continues to impress with these local favorites worth checking out:

The Tipsy Oak

This downtown gem serves up craft cocktails and elevated pub fare on one of the best patios in the city. Perfect for happy hour or weekend brunch.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery offers rotating taps of locally brewed beers in a laid-back atmosphere. Don't miss their food truck partnerships on weekends.

Hurtado Barbecue

Award-winning brisket and smoked meats that have put Arlington on the Texas BBQ map. The lines are worth it.

4 Kahunas Tiki Lounge

Transport yourself to a tropical paradise with expertly crafted tiki cocktails and island-inspired bites. A unique Arlington experience you won't find anywhere else in DFW.

Levitt Pavilion Arlington

While you're enjoying the free concerts, grab bites from the rotating food vendors at the Arlington Night Market, hosted by Small Business Mercado.

World Cup Fever Continues

With FIFA World Cup 2026 matches happening at AT&T Stadium, Arlington is buzzing with soccer fans from around the globe. Local businesses are rolling out special menus, watch parties, and themed events throughout the week.

Even if you don't have tickets to the matches, the energy around town is contagious. Head to the Entertainment District to soak in the international atmosphere.

Summer Patios Worth Visiting

Texas summer heat is no joke, but these outdoor spots make it bearable with shade, misters, and cold drinks:

Division Brewing — Covered patio with fans and plenty of seating

The Tipsy Oak — Downtown people-watching at its finest

Hurtado Barbecue — Picnic tables and that signature Texas smoke

Get Out There, Arlington

There's never been a better time to explore everything our city has to offer. From free entertainment to world-class dining, Arlington truly is The American Dream City.
