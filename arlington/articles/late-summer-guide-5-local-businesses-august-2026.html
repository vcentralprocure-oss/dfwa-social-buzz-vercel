Arlington's Late Summer Guide: 5 Local Businesses to Visit Before Fall Arrives

From award-winning barbecue to craft brews, discover the local gems making the most of summer's final weeks in The American Dream City

August 20, 2026 Arlington Pulse

Photo by Monica Bourgeau on Unsplash

As August winds down and the first hints of fall begin to whisper through The American Dream City, there's still plenty of time to soak up the last days of summer. Arlington's local business scene is buzzing with energy, offering everything from award-winning barbecue to craft brews that pair perfectly with these final warm evenings.

Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, these five local spots deserve a visit before sweater weather officially arrives.

Hurtado Barbecue

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands in a league of its own. Located in the heart of downtown Arlington, this local institution has earned its reputation as the Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers. Their slow-smoked brisket, tender ribs, and unique Tex-Mex twists have made them one of the fastest-growing independently-owned barbecue brands in the state. Don't miss their house-made sausage or the loaded brisket tacos that perfectly capture Arlington's culinary spirit.

The Tipsy Oak

For those seeking a lively atmosphere with chef-driven cuisine, The Tipsy Oak delivers on all fronts. This "Ice House" themed gastropub has become a downtown staple, offering creative cocktails, craft beer, and a menu that goes far beyond typical bar fare. Their weekend brunch draws crowds for good reason, and the patio is the perfect spot to enjoy these final weeks of summer weather. With live music regularly on the schedule, it's an ideal destination for a night out with friends.

Division Brewing

As Arlington's first locally-owned brewery, Division Brewing has been elevating the local beer scene since day one. Located on East Main Street, this craft brewery offers everything from clean, refreshing blondes to hoppy IPAs that satisfy even the most discerning palate. Their taproom provides a welcoming community space where locals gather to unwind. Check their events calendar for trivia nights and live music as summer draws to a close.

Marquez Bakery

No exploration of Arlington's local food scene is complete without a stop at Marquez Bakery. A hometown favorite for over 36 years, this family-owned gem serves up authentic Mexican pastries, homemade flour tortillas, and traditional breakfast and lunch dishes that have locals coming back generation after generation. Their tamales and pan dulce are legendary, making this the perfect spot to grab provisions for a late summer picnic or family gathering.

Inclusion Coffee

For your morning caffeine fix or an afternoon pick-me-up, Inclusion Coffee delivers specialty coffee with a community-focused mission. With their downtown location on Abram Street, they've become a gathering place for remote workers, students, and coffee enthusiasts alike. Their commitment to quality beans and expert brewing methods shows in every cup. Stop in to fuel up before exploring everything else Arlington has to offer.
