Arlington's Hidden Gems: 5 Local Businesses Worth Discovering This Weekend

From craft cocktails to artisan coffee, discover the local spots that make The American Dream City special

Photo by Courtney Rose on Unsplash

There's something special about discovering a local business that feels like it was made just for you. In Arlington, those hidden gems are everywhere—if you know where to look.

Whether you're a longtime resident or new to The American Dream City, these five local businesses offer something you won't find at the chains. They're the places where owners remember your name, where quality matters more than convenience, and where every visit feels like supporting a neighbor.

Here are five Arlington businesses worth discovering this weekend:

🍸 The Tipsy Oak

Located in the heart of downtown Arlington, The Tipsy Oak has become a local favorite for craft cocktails and elevated pub fare. Their outdoor patio is perfect for people-watching on Division Street, and their rotating seasonal menu keeps regulars coming back. Don't miss their weekend brunch—it's become something of a local institution.

☕ Nehemiah Coffee Co.

Skip the drive-thru and head to Nehemiah Coffee Co. near UTA for coffee that actually tastes like coffee should. This specialty roaster sources ethically traded beans and crafts each drink with care. The cozy atmosphere makes it ideal for remote work or catching up with friends, and their house-made pastries are the perfect companion to a morning latte.

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

Texas takes its barbecue seriously, and Hurtado Barbecue delivers some of the best in Arlington. This family-run joint has earned a loyal following for their tender brisket, house-made sausages, and sides that could be meals on their own. Get there early—they often sell out before closing time.

🥐 Marquez Bakery

For over 30 years, Marquez Bakery on Pioneer Parkway has been serving Arlington's best flour tortillas, made fresh daily using a family recipe passed down through generations. Their pan dulce selection is equally impressive, and the prices will make you wonder how they stay in business. (Spoiler: volume. Lots of volume.)

🍺 Division Brewing

Arlington's first craft brewery, Division Brewing has been pouring pints in downtown since 2015. Their taproom features a rotating selection of house-made beers, from hoppy IPAs to smooth stouts. The dog-friendly patio and regular food truck partnerships make it a weekend destination for locals in the know.

Why Local Matters

When you spend money at a local Arlington business, you're doing more than buying a product or service. You're helping a neighbor pay their mortgage. You're keeping tax dollars in our community. You're helping The American Dream City stay unique in a world of sameness.

So this weekend, skip the chains. Try one of these local spots—or better yet, try them all. Your taste buds (and your community) will thank you.
