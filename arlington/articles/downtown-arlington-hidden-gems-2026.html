5 Downtown Arlington Hidden Gems You Need to Discover This Summer

📍 Downtown Arlington • June 28, 2026 • 5 min read

Photo by Courtney Rose on Unsplash

Summer in The American Dream City is heating up, and there's no better time to explore the vibrant heart of Arlington. While everyone knows about AT&T Stadium and Six Flags, downtown Arlington holds some of the city's best-kept secrets — from craft breweries pouring local pints to vintage arcades where you can relive your childhood.

Whether you're a longtime resident or just passing through, these five downtown gems deserve a spot on your summer bucket list.

1. Division Brewing — Craft Beer with Local Soul

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery since Prohibition, Division Brewing has become a cornerstone of downtown's revival. Located on Main Street, this industrial-chic taproom serves up small-batch beers that rotate seasonally. Their outdoor patio is perfect for summer evenings, and they regularly host live music from local artists.

🍺 Division Brewing

506 E Main St, Arlington, TX
Taproom open Tuesday-Sunday with rotating food trucks

Visit Website →

2. Free Play Arcade — Retro Gaming Paradise

Step back in time at Free Play Arcade, where vintage pinball machines and classic arcade cabinets line the walls. For a flat entry fee, you get unlimited play on hundreds of games from the '80s and '90s. The downtown location also features a full bar with craft cocktails named after gaming classics.

🎮 Free Play Arcade

408 E Abram St, Arlington, TX
Unlimited play with entry • 21+ after 9pm

Visit Website →

3. Hurtado Barbecue — Texas BBQ Meets Tex-Mex

When it comes to smoked meats, Hurtado Barbecue is putting Arlington on the map. This downtown hotspot combines traditional Texas barbecue techniques with bold Tex-Mex flavors. Their brisket tacos have developed a cult following, and the house-made sausages are not to be missed. Pro tip: arrive early — they often sell out by mid-afternoon.

🥩 Hurtado Barbecue

205 E Front St, Arlington, TX
Open Wednesday-Sunday until sold out

Visit Website →

4. Theatre Arlington — Live Performances in a Historic Venue

For over 45 years, Theatre Arlington has been the cultural heartbeat of downtown. This intimate 199-seat venue produces professional-quality plays and musicals year-round. Their summer lineup typically includes family-friendly shows perfect for escaping the Texas heat. Check their calendar for upcoming performances and special events.

🎭 Theatre Arlington

305 W Main St, Arlington, TX
Season subscriptions and single tickets available

Visit Website →

5. Downtown Arlington Farmers Market — Local Flavors Every Saturday

Every Saturday morning, the Downtown Arlington Farmers Market transforms the city center into a bustling marketplace. Local farmers, artisans, and food vendors gather to sell everything from fresh produce to handmade crafts. It's the perfect way to support local businesses while stocking up on weekend essentials. The market runs year-round, rain or shine.

🥬 Downtown Arlington Farmers Market

101 W Abram St, Arlington, TX
Saturdays 8am-1pm • Year-round

Learn More →

Plan Your Downtown Adventure

Downtown Arlington is more walkable than ever, with most of these gems clustered within a few blocks of each other. Start with coffee at a local café, spend the afternoon gaming or catching a show, then cap off your evening with craft beer and barbecue. The First Thursdays event series (monthly block parties) adds even more energy to the area — check the Downtown Arlington website for upcoming dates.
