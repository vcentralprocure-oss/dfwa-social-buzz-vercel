Your Arlington Coffee & Weekend Guide: 5 Local Spots to Explore

From specialty coffee roasters to scenic parks and award-winning BBQ, discover the local gems making Arlington's weekend scene shine.

August 7, 2026

Arlington's coffee culture is having a moment, and locals couldn't be more excited. From specialty roasters to cozy neighborhood cafés, The American Dream City is brewing up something special for caffeine enthusiasts.

Nehemiah Coffee Co

Located in the heart of downtown Arlington, this roaster sources ethically traded beans and crafts each cup with precision. Their pour-over bar is a must-try for serious coffee lovers, and the welcoming atmosphere makes it an ideal spot for remote work or casual meetups.

Inclusion Coffee

Just a short drive away, Inclusion Coffee lives up to its name by creating a space where everyone feels welcome. Their seasonal menu rotates regularly, featuring creative lattes that showcase local flavors. The community tables and friendly baristas have made this spot a favorite among UTA students and young professionals.

Arlington Coffee Roasters

For those seeking a true Texas coffee experience, Arlington Coffee Roasters delivers with small-batch roasting that highlights the unique characteristics of each bean origin. Their tasting notes are educational without being pretentious, making specialty coffee accessible to newcomers and connoisseurs alike.

River Legacy Parks

Beyond coffee, Arlington's weekend scene offers plenty to explore. River Legacy Parks provides over 1,300 acres of natural beauty perfect for hiking, biking, or simply unwinding after your morning brew. The trails wind through dense forest along the Trinity River, offering a peaceful escape from city life.

Hurtado Barbecue

When hunger strikes, head to Hurtado Barbecue on Division Street. This local favorite has earned a reputation for perfectly smoked brisket and house-made sausages. The no-frills approach lets the quality of the meat speak for itself, and the line out the door proves Arlington locals know where to find the best BBQ in town.

Whether you're a coffee aficionado or simply looking for a new weekend routine, Arlington's local businesses are ready to welcome you. Support local, drink good coffee, and discover why The American Dream City is becoming a destination for those who appreciate authentic community spots.
