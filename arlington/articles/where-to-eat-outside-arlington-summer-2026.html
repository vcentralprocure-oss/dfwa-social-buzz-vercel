Where to Eat Outside in Arlington This Summer: 5 Patios You Can't Miss

From downtown hideaways to Entertainment District gems, these outdoor dining spots are perfect for soaking up the Texas sunshine.

June 3, 2026

Photo by Tim Stief on Unsplash

Summer has officially arrived in Arlington, and there's no better way to enjoy the warm Texas evenings than dining al fresco. Whether you're looking for a romantic date night spot, a family-friendly patio, or a place to catch up with friends over craft cocktails, Arlington's restaurant scene has you covered.

We've rounded up five of the best patios in The American Dream City where you can enjoy great food, refreshing drinks, and that unbeatable outdoor atmosphere.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Craft Cocktails Meet Cozy Outdoor Vibes

If you haven't experienced The Tipsy Oak yet, you're missing out on one of Arlington's best-kept secrets. Located in the heart of downtown, this gastropub features a sprawling patio that's perfect for summer evenings.

Their craft cocktail menu rotates seasonally, and their outdoor seating area is dotted with string lights that create a magical atmosphere as the sun goes down. The patio fills up fast on weekends, so arrive early or make a reservation.

Division Brewing: Arlington's Original Craft Brewery

For beer lovers, Division Brewing is a must-visit destination. As Arlington's first locally-owned brewery, they've built a loyal following with their innovative brews and welcoming taproom.

Their outdoor seating area is spacious and dog-friendly, making it the perfect spot to enjoy a cold one with your four-legged friend. Try their flagship IPA or ask about their rotating seasonal offerings. The laid-back vibe here is quintessentially Arlington.

Hurtado Barbecue: Award-Winning BBQ Under the Texas Sky

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands out from the crowd. This award-winning spot has earned national recognition for their perfectly smoked meats and creative sides.

Their outdoor seating area lets you enjoy that mouthwatering brisket and house-made sausages in the open air. The casual, picnic-style setup is ideal for families and groups. Don't skip the loaded baked potato or the banana pudding for dessert.

Babe's Chicken Dinner House: Classic Texas Comfort on the Patio

No list of Arlington dining spots would be complete without mentioning Babe's Chicken Dinner House. This Texas institution serves up legendary fried chicken and all the fixings in a charming, rustic setting.

Their patio seating offers a relaxed atmosphere where you can dig into bottomless sides and watch the world go by. It's comfort food at its finest, and the outdoor dining experience makes it even better during summer months.

Pony Up: Entertainment District Energy with Outdoor Flair

Located in the bustling Entertainment District, Pony Up brings serious energy to Arlington's dining scene. Their creative takes on classic American fare pair perfectly with their craft cocktail program.

The outdoor seating area puts you right in the middle of the action, with views of the nearby stadiums and entertainment venues. It's the perfect pre-game or post-concert spot, and the people-watching is unmatched.

Why Arlington's Patio Scene Matters

Arlington's outdoor dining culture isn't just about good food—it's about community. These patios serve as gathering places where neighbors meet, friendships form, and memories are made. In a city that's constantly growing and evolving, these outdoor spaces help maintain that small-town Texas feel that makes Arlington special.

From downtown's revitalized Urban Union District to the energy of the Entertainment District, Arlington's restaurant patios reflect the city's unique character. Each spot offers something different, but they all share that warm Texas hospitality that keeps locals coming back.

So grab your sunglasses, round up your crew, and explore these fantastic outdoor dining options. Your perfect summer evening in Arlington is waiting.
