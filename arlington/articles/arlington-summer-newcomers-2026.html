What's New in Arlington: Summer 2026 Openings You Need to Know

From Blue Mint Thai to Rocketbelly boba, discover the exciting new businesses bringing fresh flavors to The American Dream City this summer.

June 11, 2026

Arlington's dining scene continues to evolve with exciting new openings this summer.

Summer 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most exciting seasons for Arlington's local business scene. With the FIFA World Cup bringing global attention to our city, entrepreneurs are seizing the moment to introduce fresh concepts that showcase the diversity and creativity defining modern Arlington.

Whether you're a longtime resident or one of the thousands of visitors expected to explore The American Dream City this summer, these new openings deserve a spot on your must-visit list.

Blue Mint Thai Brings Authentic Flavors to Downtown

One of the most anticipated openings of the season, Blue Mint Thai is set to become Arlington's go-to destination for authentic Thai cuisine. This locally owned restaurant promises to deliver the kind of flavorful, carefully crafted dishes that have made Thai food a national favorite—from perfectly balanced pad thai to aromatic curries that transport your taste buds straight to Bangkok.

Blue Mint Thai

Locally owned authentic Thai restaurant opening summer 2026 in Downtown Arlington. Expect traditional favorites made with fresh ingredients and time-honored recipes.

Rocketbelly: Boba and Sweet Treats Land in Arlington

For those with a sweet tooth, Rocketbelly is launching just in time for the Texas heat. This innovative boba shop combines refreshing drinks with indulgent sweet treats, creating the perfect afternoon pick-me-up or post-dinner destination. Their creative approach to bubble tea—featuring house-made syrups and unique flavor combinations—sets them apart from the typical chain experience.

Rocketbelly

A fresh boba shop concept serving refreshing drinks and sweet treats. Opening summer 2026 with creative bubble tea flavors and house-made ingredients.

Inclusion Coffee: Community-Focused Café Culture

Also joining the Downtown Arlington lineup is Inclusion Coffee, a community-focused café that's already generating buzz among local coffee enthusiasts. Located at 101 Center alongside Rita's Italian Ice and Frost Bank, Inclusion Coffee represents the kind of thoughtful, people-centered business that helps define great neighborhoods.

Inclusion Coffee

Community-focused coffee shop opening at 101 Center in Downtown Arlington. Part of a vibrant new development bringing multiple businesses to the area.

World Cup Energy Powers Arlington's Growth

These openings aren't happening in a vacuum. Arlington is preparing to welcome the world as FIFA World Cup matches arrive at AT&T Stadium. The city has been busy with infrastructure improvements, including a new retro-themed hotel and the demolition of aging landmarks to make way for fresh development.

This wave of new businesses reflects a broader confidence in Arlington's future. From the Urban Union District expansion to the ongoing revitalization of Downtown, entrepreneurs are betting big on The American Dream City—and residents are the winners.
