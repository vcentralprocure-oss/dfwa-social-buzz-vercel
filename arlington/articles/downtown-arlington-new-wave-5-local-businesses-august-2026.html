Downtown Arlington's New Wave: 5 Must-Visit Local Businesses This August

August 16, 2026 • Arlington Pulse

Photo by Fairuz Naufal Zaki on Unsplash

There's something special happening in Downtown Arlington.

Over the past year, our city's urban core has transformed from a quiet business district into a vibrant destination where locals gather, connect, and discover something new around every corner. Whether you're a lifelong resident or just passing through, these five local businesses showcase why Arlington's downtown renaissance is worth experiencing.

Daydream Bookstore Cafe

Imagine stepping into a Hallmark movie scene—except it's real, and it's right here at 380 E. Front St. Daydream is America's romance-only bookstore, pairing curated love stories with specialty coffee in a space designed for lingering. Browse shelves organized by tropes (enemies-to-lovers, anyone?), sip a lavender latte, and lose yourself in a world where happily-ever-afters are guaranteed. It's the kind of place that makes you believe in magic—and Arlington's creative future.

Visit Daydream Bookstore Cafe →

Hurtado Barbecue

Texas takes its BBQ seriously, and Hurtado Barbecue on Division Street delivers seriously good eats. This local institution has earned its reputation through slow-smoked brisket, house-made sausages, and sides that could steal the show. The line out the door isn't hype—it's hungry locals who know that some things are worth waiting for. Grab a tray, load up on jalapeño cheddar links, and taste why Arlington's food scene is getting statewide attention.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

Inclusion Coffee

More than just a coffee shop, Inclusion Coffee has become a community cornerstone where everyone belongs. Their ethically sourced beans are roasted to perfection, but it's the welcoming atmosphere that keeps regulars coming back. From students studying for finals to professionals taking Zoom calls, this downtown spot proves that great coffee and genuine hospitality go hand in hand. Don't miss their seasonal drinks—summer's peach cold brew is a game-changer.

Visit Inclusion Coffee →

The Tipsy Oak

When happy hour calls, The Tipsy Oak answers with craft cocktails, local brews, and a patio that's perfect for people-watching. Located in the heart of downtown, this neighborhood bar strikes the balance between upscale and approachable. Their rotating cocktail menu features Texas spirits, while the kitchen serves up elevated pub fare that pairs perfectly with your drink of choice. It's where Arlington comes to unwind—and where you'll probably run into someone you know.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

Marquez Bakery & Tortilla Factory

No list of Arlington essentials is complete without mentioning Marquez Bakery. For generations, this family-run institution has been producing tortillas so good they've developed a cult following. The bakery case overflows with pan dulce, empanadas, and Mexican pastries that transport you straight to abuela's kitchen. Come early—these treasures sell out fast, and locals know it.

Visit Marquez Bakery →

Why Downtown Matters

These five businesses represent more than great products—they're proof that Arlington is building something authentic. In an era of chains and franchises, our downtown entrepreneurs are taking risks, creating jobs, and giving The American Dream City a character all its own.

So this weekend, skip the drive-thru. Park on Front Street, grab a coffee, browse a bookstore, and remember why you chose to call Arlington home.
