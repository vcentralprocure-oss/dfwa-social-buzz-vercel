Your Arlington Back-to-School Guide: 5 Local Businesses Ready to Help

August 10, 2026 · Arlington Pulse Staff

Photo by Unsplash

As summer winds down and the first day of school approaches, Arlington families are shifting gears from vacation mode to preparation mode. With Arlington ISD classes starting August 12th, now is the perfect time to discover the local businesses that make back-to-school season smoother, more affordable, and even a little fun.

From school supply savings to family-friendly dining spots perfect for those busy weeknights, these five Arlington businesses have your back as you gear up for the new academic year.

Target: Your One-Stop School Supply Destination

When it comes to crossing everything off that school supply list, Target remains Arlington's go-to destination. With locations across the city including the popular Arlington Highlands store, Target offers competitive prices on everything from backpacks and lunch boxes to calculators and art supplies.

Their annual back-to-school sales can save families significant money, and the REDcard offers an additional 5% off every purchase. Plus, with same-day delivery and order pickup options, busy parents can shop on their schedule.

Walmart: Budget-Friendly Basics for Every Student

For families watching their budgets, Walmart delivers unbeatable value on back-to-school essentials. Their Arlington locations, including the Supercenter on East Lamar Boulevard, stock everything from uniforms and shoes to electronics and dorm room necessities.

Walmart's price match guarantee ensures you're getting the best deal, and their app makes it easy to check inventory before you head out. Don't miss their rollback prices on bulk items like notebooks, pens, and folders.

The Tipsy Oak: Family Dining Made Easy

Once school starts, weeknight dinners become a logistical challenge. The Tipsy Oak on Abram Street solves this problem with their family-friendly atmosphere and menu that pleases both kids and parents.

Their outdoor patio is perfect for those warm August evenings, and their weekly specials make dining out affordable even during the expensive back-to-school season. From craft burgers to fresh salads, there's something for every palate in your family.

Half Price Books: Fuel Young Minds for Less

Reading is fundamental to academic success, and Half Price Books on South Cooper Street makes building a home library affordable. Their vast selection of new and used books covers every reading level and interest.

During back-to-school season, they often run special promotions on educational materials, reference books, and children's literature. It's the perfect place to find supplemental reading materials or reward your young scholar with a new book.

Marquez Bakery: Fuel Up with Authentic Breakfast

Before those early morning school drop-offs, stop by Marquez Bakery on Pioneer Parkway for authentic Mexican breakfast items that will fuel your family's day. Their fresh tortillas, breakfast tacos, and pan dulce are local favorites.

Grab a dozen tortillas for the week or treat the kids to fresh pastries before that first day of school. This family-owned institution has been serving Arlington for generations and understands the importance of starting the day right.

Make This School Year Your Best Yet

Back-to-school season doesn't have to be stressful. By supporting these Arlington businesses, you're not just checking items off your to-do list—you're investing in our local community while setting your family up for academic success.

From all of us at Arlington Pulse, here's to a fantastic school year ahead!
