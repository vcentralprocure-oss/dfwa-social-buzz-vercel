Arlington's August Guide: 5 Local Businesses to Visit Before Summer Ends

From craft breweries to family-friendly dining, discover where locals are spending their final weeks of summer in The American Dream City

📅 August 11, 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read

Photo by Carlos Delgado on Unsplash

Summer is winding down in Arlington, but there's still plenty of time to explore the local businesses that make our city special. Whether you're looking for a place to unwind after back-to-school shopping or want to savor the last warm evenings on a patio, these five Arlington spots deserve a visit before fall arrives.

The Tipsy Oak

This downtown favorite has become the go-to gathering spot for Arlington locals. With its expansive patio, live music on weekends, and chef-driven menu, The Tipsy Oak captures everything great about our city's growing food scene. Their classic burgers and signature Texas Mule have earned a loyal following, and the ice-house atmosphere makes it perfect for casual summer evenings.

Hurtado Barbecue

When it comes to Texas barbecue, Hurtado Barbecue stands out as one of Arlington's crown jewels. This locally-owned spot has garnered attention far beyond our city limits for its perfectly smoked brisket and authentic Texas flavors. Located near the heart of downtown, it's the perfect place to grab a hearty meal before exploring the area.

Division Brewing

Arlington's craft beer scene continues to grow, and Division Brewing leads the charge. With rotating seasonal brews and a welcoming taproom atmosphere, this local brewery has become a community hub. Their outdoor seating area is ideal for enjoying those final summer nights with friends.

Marquez Bakery

No guide to Arlington would be complete without mentioning Marquez Bakery. This family-run institution has been serving fresh tortillas and authentic Mexican baked goods for generations. Stop by for breakfast tacos in the morning or grab a bag of their famous tortillas to take home.

Rocketbelly

For something refreshingly different, check out Rocketbelly in downtown Arlington. As Texas' first self-serve boba bar, they've brought a unique twist to the beverage scene. Customize your drink exactly how you like it – perfect for cooling off during these last hot days of August.

Why Supporting Local Matters

These businesses represent more than just great food and drinks – they're the backbone of Arlington's economy and community identity. Each one contributes to the unique character that makes The American Dream City a place worth calling home.

As you plan your final summer outings, consider making one of these local spots your destination. Your support helps ensure Arlington's independent business scene continues to thrive.
