Arlington's Entertainment District: Your Ultimate Summer Guide to Dining, Games & Fun

From Rangers baseball to rooftop cocktails, discover why locals and visitors alike flock to Arlington's most exciting neighborhood

May 11, 2026

Photo by Daniel on Unsplash

There's a reason they call it the Entertainment Capital of Texas. Arlington's Entertainment District isn't just a collection of attractions—it's a vibrant ecosystem where world-class sports, thrilling theme parks, and an exploding food scene come together to create experiences you simply can't find anywhere else in DFW.

Whether you're catching a Rangers game under the retractable roof at Globe Life Field, screaming your way through Six Flags, or discovering your new favorite restaurant on a hidden patio, this neighborhood delivers non-stop energy from morning till late night. Here's your insider guide to making the most of Arlington's Entertainment District this summer.

Where to Eat: The Entertainment District's Best Bites

The food scene around Globe Life Field and AT&T Stadium has evolved far beyond typical stadium fare. Today's Entertainment District is home to chef-driven restaurants, craft breweries, and local institutions that draw crowds even when there's no game in town.

Start your culinary tour at Texas Live!, the sprawling dining and entertainment complex that anchors the district. With multiple restaurants under one roof, you can go from Texas barbecue at Pudge's Pizza to upscale Southern fare at the Arlington Backyard. The complex really comes alive on game days, when the outdoor plaza fills with fans celebrating pre-game.

Visit Texas Live!

For a more intimate dining experience, head to Babe's Chicken Dinner House on Division Street. This local legend has been serving up family-style fried chicken and all the fixings since 1981. The wait can be long on weekends, but that's just proof that Arlington locals know where to find the good stuff. Come hungry—the portions are legendary.

Visit Babe's Chicken Dinner House

When you're craving something lighter, Pluckers Wing Bar near the stadium delivers on their promise of "wings, beer, and sports." Their signature "Dirty Bird" wings have developed a cult following, and the rooftop patio offers surprisingly good views of the surrounding area. It's become a go-to spot for post-game celebrations.

Visit Pluckers Wing Bar

Game Day Experiences: Beyond the Stadium

Sure, catching a Rangers game at Globe Life Field is the main event—but the Entertainment District offers plenty of ways to extend the experience. The stadium itself is worth exploring, with its retractable roof, air-conditioned concourses, and impressive collection of Texas baseball memorabilia.

Before first pitch, join the crowds at Luckytown Brewing, Arlington's first craft brewery located just minutes from the stadium. Their rotating tap list features everything from crisp lagers perfect for Texas heat to bold IPAs that stand up to barbecue. The taproom has a laid-back vibe that welcomes both serious beer enthusiasts and casual fans just looking for a cold one before the game.

Visit Luckytown Brewing

If you're visiting with family, don't miss the opportunity to explore Six Flags Over Texas before or after your game. The iconic theme park has been thrilling visitors since 1961, and recent additions like the Harley Quinn Spinsanity coaster keep the experience fresh for repeat visitors. Pro tip: the park offers discounted evening admission that's perfect for catching a few rides after a day game.

Visit Six Flags Over Texas

Where to Stay: Making It a Weekend

With so much to do in the Entertainment District, many visitors are choosing to make it a multi-day experience. The Live! by Loews hotel puts you right in the heart of the action, with rooms overlooking Globe Life Field and direct access to Texas Live! The property has become a destination in itself, with a resort-style pool and multiple dining options.

Visit Live! by Loews Arlington

For a more budget-friendly option, the Hilton Arlington offers comfortable accommodations just a short walk from both stadiums. Their shuttle service to local attractions makes it easy to explore without worrying about parking.

Summer Events You Can't Miss

The Entertainment District really shines during the summer months, when extended daylight hours and warm evenings create the perfect backdrop for outdoor events. The Arlington Backyard at Texas Live! hosts regular concerts featuring both national acts and local favorites, with the outdoor stage creating an electric atmosphere under the stars.

Movie nights at Levitt Pavilion in nearby downtown Arlington offer a free, family-friendly alternative to the high-energy stadium scene. Bring a blanket, grab dinner from one of the food trucks, and enjoy classic films on the big screen with the downtown skyline as your backdrop.

Whether you're a die-hard sports fan, a thrill-seeker looking for your next adrenaline rush, or a foodie on the hunt for your next favorite meal, Arlington's Entertainment District delivers. This summer, skip the usual DFW haunts and discover why this neighborhood has become the region's premier destination for entertainment.
