Summer Food & Drink Guide: 5 Arlington Local Gems You Can't Miss

June 30, 2026 • Arlington Pulse • Local Business Spotlight
Photo by Carlos Delgado on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in The American Dream City, and there's no better time to explore Arlington's vibrant local food and drink scene. From award-winning barbecue to craft breweries that have put our city on the map, these five local gems represent the best of what Arlington has to offer.

Whether you're a longtime resident looking for new favorites or visiting for a Rangers game at Globe Life Field, this guide will lead you to the spots that locals keep coming back to.

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

The Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers

Hurtado Barbecue has become one of the fastest-growing independently-owned barbecue brands in Texas, and for good reason. Located in the heart of downtown Arlington at 205 E Front St, they combine Central Texas smoking techniques with a distinctive Tex-Mex accent that sets them apart.

Must-try: The brisket, ribs, and their famous birria tacos. Don't skip the sides—locals rave about the cherry bomb and Texas twinkie.

Visit Hurtado Barbecue →

🍺 Division Brewing

Arlington's First Locally-Owned Brewery

Division Brewing holds a special place in Arlington's history as the city's first locally-owned brewery. Located at 506 E Main St, they've elevated the beer drinker's experience with unique flavors ranging from clean, refreshing light ales to bold, hoppy India pale ales.

The vibe: A welcoming taproom that hosts regular events including trivia nights and live music. It's the perfect spot to unwind after exploring downtown.

Visit Division Brewing →

🍳 The Tipsy Oak

Where Comfort Food Meets Craft Cocktails

The Tipsy Oak has quickly become one of Arlington's most beloved restaurants, consistently ranking among the top spots on local review sites. This gastropub offers a creative menu that pairs elevated comfort food with an impressive selection of craft cocktails and local brews.

Why locals love it: The perfect balance of casual atmosphere and quality cuisine makes it ideal for everything from date nights to family dinners.

Visit The Tipsy Oak →

🍔 Rodeo Goat

Creative Burgers in a Fun Atmosphere

Rodeo Goat has earned a reputation for serving some of the most creative and delicious burgers in Arlington. With a playful atmosphere and a menu that pushes boundaries, it's a must-visit for burger enthusiasts looking for something beyond the ordinary.

What to expect: Unique burger combinations, a great selection of craft beers, and a lively environment that captures the spirit of Texas.

Visit Rodeo Goat →

🥩 The Original Roy Hutchins Barbeque

A Legacy of Texas BBQ Excellence

The Original Roy Hutchins Barbeque represents the traditional side of Arlington's barbecue scene. This establishment has built its reputation on time-honored smoking techniques and a commitment to quality that keeps barbecue purists coming back for more.

The experience: Authentic Texas barbecue served with the kind of hospitality that makes you feel like family.

Visit Roy Hutchins BBQ →

Plan Your Culinary Adventure

These five establishments represent just a taste of what Arlington's food scene has to offer. From the innovative Tex-Mex barbecue fusion at Hurtado to the craft beer pioneers at Division Brewing, each spot tells a story about our city's growing culinary identity.

Make it a weekend adventure—start with lunch at Hurtado, grab afternoon drinks at Division Brewing, and end your evening with dinner at The Tipsy Oak. You'll experience the full spectrum of flavors that make Arlington a destination for food lovers.
