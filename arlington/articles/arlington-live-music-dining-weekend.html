Arlington's Live Music & Dining Scene: Your Weekend Guide to the Best Venues

By Arlington Pulse | May 16, 2026 | Music, Dining & Entertainment

There's something electric happening in Arlington's music scene right now. From intimate acoustic sets at downtown gastropubs to high-energy performances at the Entertainment District, live music has become the heartbeat of our city's nightlife. Whether you're planning a date night, catching up with friends, or simply want to discover your new favorite local artist, this weekend delivers an unbeatable lineup of venues where great food meets even better tunes.

What makes Arlington's live music scene special isn't just the talent—it's the variety. You can catch country covers at a honky-tonk, jazz trios at a wine bar, and indie rock bands at converted warehouses, all within a few miles of each other. And the best part? Most of these spots serve food that's just as impressive as their playlists.

Where to Catch Live Music This Weekend

Texas Live! ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The crown jewel of Arlington's Entertainment District, Texas Live! isn't just a venue—it's an experience. With multiple stages, outdoor patios, and a constant rotation of local and touring acts, this is where you go when you want energy. This weekend features everything from country cover bands to DJ sets that keep the dance floor packed until closing.

Don't miss: The outdoor stage when the weather's nice—there's nothing quite like live music under the Texas stars with a cold drink in hand.

Check their event calendar →

Hearsay Arlington ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Downtown Arlington's most sophisticated live music destination, Hearsay combines upscale Southern cuisine with an intimate listening room that hosts everything from jazz quartets to acoustic singer-songwriters. The exposed brick walls and warm lighting create the perfect backdrop for a romantic dinner or a relaxed evening with friends.

Pro tip: Make reservations for dinner, then linger for the late set. Their craft cocktail menu is worth the trip alone.

Reserve your table →

Arlington Music Hall ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This historic venue has been hosting performances since 1950, and walking through its doors feels like stepping into a piece of Texas entertainment history. From country legends to up-and-coming indie acts, the Music Hall offers a more traditional concert experience with incredible acoustics and an intimate setting that puts you close to the stage no matter where you sit.

See upcoming shows →

Dining Spots with Live Entertainment

Levitt Pavilion Arlington ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Perhaps the best deal in town—Levitt Pavilion offers 50+ free concerts every year in their beautiful outdoor amphitheater. Pack a picnic, bring a blanket, and enjoy everything from Latin jazz to indie folk under the stars. The surrounding food trucks and nearby downtown restaurants make it easy to grab dinner before the show.

View the free concert schedule →

J. Gilligan's Bar & Grill ⭐⭐⭐⭐

A downtown institution known for their Irish nachos and lively atmosphere, J. Gilligan's regularly hosts local bands on their outdoor patio. It's the kind of place where you can catch a casual acoustic set while enjoying comfort food and cold beer with friends.

Visit their website →

Planning Your Music-Filled Weekend

The beauty of Arlington's live music scene is that you don't have to choose between dinner and a show—you can have both. Start with happy hour at Hearsay, catch an early set at J. Gilligan's, then head to Texas Live! for the late-night energy. Or keep it simple with a picnic dinner at Levitt Pavilion and a free concert under the stars.

Whatever your vibe, Arlington's venues are ready to deliver. Just remember to check event calendars in advance—popular shows can sell out, and some venues require reservations for dinner seating during performances.
