Your Ultimate Guide to Arlington's Best Local Restaurants This Summer

From award-winning barbecue to craft brewery bites, discover why Arlington's food scene is the talk of DFW

Published: May 24, 2026 | Arlington Pulse

Arlington's culinary scene is having a serious moment. While the city has long been known for its world-class entertainment venues like AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field, locals know the real magic happens in the restaurants, breweries, and hidden gems scattered across town.

This summer, there's never been a better time to explore what Arlington's food scene has to offer. From Texas-style barbecue that's drawing crowds from across the state to craft breweries serving up innovative brews alongside chef-driven menus, here's your guide to the spots that should be on every local's radar.

Hurtado Barbecue: The Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers

If there's one name you need to know in Arlington barbecue, it's Hurtado Barbecue. What started as pop-up events across North Texas has exploded into one of the most talked-about BBQ joints in DFW. The original Arlington location, which opened in 2020 in Downtown Arlington, has become a destination for serious barbecue enthusiasts.

Brandon Hurtado and his team have earned their reputation through painstaking attention to the pit. Their brisket achieves that perfect balance of smoke, bark, and tenderness that Texas barbecue dreams are made of. But it's not just about the meat—their sides and house-made sausages have developed cult followings of their own.

Pro tip: Hurtado Barbecue is also the Official BBQ of the Texas Rangers, so you can grab their legendary smoked meats inside Globe Life Field at Arlington Eats in Section 101 during every home game.

Division Brewing: Arlington's First Locally-Owned Brewery

When Division Brewing opened its doors on East Main Street, it marked a milestone for Arlington's craft beer scene. As the city's first locally-owned brewery, Division has been elevating the beer drinker's experience with unique flavors that range from clean, refreshing light ales to bold, complex stouts and fruity sour beers.

The taproom has become a community gathering spot where locals come together over pints of carefully crafted brews. With regular events like trivia nights and live music, Division Brewing isn't just a place to grab a beer—it's become part of the fabric of Downtown Arlington.

Their outdoor patio is perfect for those warm Texas evenings, and the rotating tap list means there's always something new to discover. Whether you're a hop-head chasing the latest IPA or prefer something lighter and more sessionable, Division has you covered.

Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery featuring craft beers, trivia nights, live music, and a great outdoor patio.

The Tipsy Oak: Where Craft Beer Meets Chef-Driven Cuisine

Located in the heart of Downtown Arlington, The Tipsy Oak has carved out a unique niche as a gastropub-icehouse hybrid that delivers serious food alongside an impressive beer and cocktail program. This isn't your typical bar food—the chef-driven menu features creative takes on comfort classics that pair perfectly with their craft beer selection.

The Tipsy Oak has become a go-to spot for locals looking for a relaxed atmosphere without sacrificing quality. Their live music lineup adds to the vibrant energy, making it a perfect destination for date nights, group gatherings, or casual weeknight dinners.

The full bar means cocktail enthusiasts will find plenty to love, while wine drinkers and beer fans have curated selections to explore. It's the kind of place where you can come for happy hour and end up staying for dinner—and then dessert.

The Tipsy Oak

Downtown Arlington gastropub featuring chef-driven cuisine, craft beer, cocktails, and live music.

Babe's Chicken Dinner House: A Texas Tradition

No guide to Arlington dining would be complete without mentioning Babe's Chicken Dinner House. Since opening in November 2010, this Arlington location has been serving up the kind of honest-to-goodness comfort food that Texas families have loved for generations.

Babe's isn't trying to reinvent the wheel—they're just doing classic Southern fare exceptionally well. The fried chicken is crispy, juicy, and utterly addictive. The sides taste like they came straight from grandma's kitchen. And the atmosphere? It's exactly what you'd want from a family dinner house—warm, welcoming, and unpretentious.

What makes Babe's special is their commitment to the experience. This is a sit-down restaurant where people really feel at home. The friendly service, generous portions, and consistent quality have made it a beloved institution for Arlington families.

Babe's Chicken Dinner House

Texas tradition since 2010 serving legendary fried chicken and Southern comfort food in a family-friendly atmosphere.

Restaurant506: Fine Dining in an Exquisite Setting

For those special occasions when you want to elevate your dining experience, Restaurant506 at The Sanford House Inn & Spa delivers contemporary American cuisine in one of Arlington's most elegant settings. This is where locals go to celebrate anniversaries, birthdays, and milestones—or simply to treat themselves to an exceptional meal.

The menu changes with the seasons, showcasing the best ingredients available through creative, beautifully plated dishes. The attentive service and refined atmosphere make every visit feel like an occasion, whether you're enjoying a romantic dinner for two or a celebratory meal with friends.

Don't skip dessert—their salted caramel tart has developed a reputation as one of the best sweet endings in Arlington. And with its location at The Sanford House, you can easily turn dinner into a weekend getaway.

Fine dining at The Sanford House featuring contemporary American cuisine, seasonal menus, and an elegant atmosphere.

Why Arlington's Food Scene Matters

What makes Arlington's restaurant scene special isn't just the quality of the food—it's the diversity of experiences available within a few square miles. You can go from down-home barbecue to refined fine dining, from craft brewery casual to family-style comfort food, all without leaving the city limits.

These restaurants aren't just feeding Arlington—they're helping define what it means to be a local in this city. They sponsor Little League teams, host community events, and create the gathering spaces where neighbors become friends.

So this summer, skip the chains and explore what makes Arlington's food scene truly special. Your taste buds will thank you.
