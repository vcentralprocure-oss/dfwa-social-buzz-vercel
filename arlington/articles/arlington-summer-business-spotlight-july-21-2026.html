Arlington's Summer Business Spotlight: 5 Local Gems to Discover This Week

July 21, 2026 • Arlington Pulse

Photo by Tim Stief on Unsplash

July in Arlington brings more than just triple-digit temperatures—it brings opportunities to discover the local businesses that make our community special. While the World Cup excitement may be winding down, the entrepreneurial spirit in The American Dream City is just heating up.

Marquez Bakery: A Division Street Institution

For over three decades, Marquez Bakery has been a cornerstone of East Division Street, serving as a bakery, restaurant, and tortilla factory all under one roof. This family-owned establishment has earned its reputation through fresh, handmade tortillas and authentic Mexican baked goods that keep locals coming back generation after generation. Their breakfast tacos and pan dulce have become morning rituals for Arlington residents who know that freshness matters.

Taste of Europe: Authentic Flavors in the Heart of Texas

When you're craving something beyond the typical Texas fare, Taste of Europe delivers an authentic Eastern European experience that transports diners across the Atlantic. From pierogi to schnitzel, this local gem has built a loyal following among Arlington's diverse community. Their commitment to traditional recipes and quality ingredients has made them a standout in our city's growing international food scene.

The Tipsy Oak: Downtown's Social Hub

No roundup of Arlington businesses would be complete without mentioning The Tipsy Oak. This downtown staple has mastered the art of the neighborhood gathering place, offering craft cocktails, local brews, and an expansive patio that's perfect for summer evenings. Whether you're meeting friends after work or looking for a weekend brunch spot, The Tipsy Oak delivers the kind of welcoming atmosphere that defines Arlington's community spirit.

Hurtado Barbecue: Smoked Meat Perfection

Texas BBQ is serious business, and Hurtado Barbecue has earned its place among the best in DFW. Their post oak-smoked brisket has developed a cult following, with lines forming early on weekends as locals and visitors alike wait for a taste of their perfectly rendered meat. The combination of traditional techniques and creative sides makes this a must-visit for anyone who appreciates authentic Texas barbecue.

Division Brewing: Community in Every Pour

As Arlington's first craft brewery, Division Brewing has been at the forefront of the city's craft beer revolution. Their taproom serves as a community hub where locals gather to enjoy everything from crisp lagers to hazy IPAs. The brewery's commitment to using local ingredients and supporting community events has made them more than just a place to grab a beer—they're a vital part of Arlington's social fabric.

Why Local Matters

These five businesses represent just a fraction of what makes Arlington special. Each one contributes to the unique character of our city, creating jobs, supporting local suppliers, and building the kind of community connections that chain establishments simply can't replicate.

As we move through the summer months, consider making an intentional effort to support these local gems. Whether it's grabbing breakfast at Marquez Bakery, enjoying dinner at Taste of Europe, or meeting friends for drinks at The Tipsy Oak or Division Brewing, your dollars stay in the community and help ensure these businesses continue thriving.

The American Dream City earned its name through the entrepreneurial spirit of business owners like these. By supporting them, you're not just getting great food and drinks—you're investing in Arlington's future.
