This Weekend in Arlington: Farmers Market, Live Music & Summer Fun

June 4, 2026

Photo by Edward Lawrence on Unsplash

Summer is in full swing in The American Dream City, and this weekend is packed with can't-miss events. Whether you're a longtime local or just visiting, here's your complete guide to making the most of June 6-7 in Arlington.

Saturday Morning: Downtown Farmers Market

Start your weekend at the Downtown Arlington Farmers Market, now running every Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM at the corner of Front and Center Streets. This community gem features fresh, locally-sourced produce, handcrafted artisan goods, and that small-town charm that makes Arlington special.

Entry and parking are completely free, making it the perfect family-friendly outing. Follow their updates on Instagram to see which vendors are featured each week.

Saturday Afternoon: Explore Downtown's Best

After the market, stick around Downtown Arlington to discover some of the city's most beloved local businesses. Here are five spots you won't want to miss:

🍺 Division Brewing

Arlington's first locally-owned brewery serves up unique craft beers from refreshing blondes to fruity sours. Their outdoor patio is perfect for summer afternoons.

Visit Website →

🍖 Hurtado Barbecue

What started as a pop-up serving brewery-goers has become one of DFW's most talked-about BBQ spots. Brandon and Hannah Hurtado bring authentic Texas smoke right to Division Brewing.

Visit Website →

🍴 The Tipsy Oak

This downtown gastropub combines chef-driven menus with craft beer, wine, and live music. Their patio is one of the best people-watching spots in the city.

Visit Website →

☕ White Rhino Coffee

Need a caffeine boost? This local favorite serves artisan coffee in a welcoming atmosphere that's become a hub for UTA students and remote workers alike.

Visit Website →

🎨 Wondrous Works

Discover unique gifts, handmade treasures, and vintage finds at this downtown boutique that showcases local artisans and makers.

Visit Website →

Sunday: Relax & Recharge

Sunday in Arlington is all about taking it easy. Grab brunch at one of downtown's cozy cafés, take a stroll through River Legacy Parks for some nature therapy, or catch a matinee at the Studio Movie Grill in the Entertainment District.

Looking Ahead

With World Cup matches coming to AT&T Stadium later this month, Arlington is about to be in the global spotlight. Now's the perfect time to explore your own backyard and discover why The American Dream City is becoming one of Texas's most exciting destinations.
