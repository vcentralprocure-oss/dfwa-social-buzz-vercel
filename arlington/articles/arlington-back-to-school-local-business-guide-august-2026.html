Back to School in Arlington: Your Guide to Local Shops & Services

From school supply savings to family-friendly restaurants, discover Arlington businesses ready to help your family start the school year strong.

August 8, 2026

With Arlington ISD students heading back to classrooms on August 12, families across The American Dream City are gearing up for another school year. But beyond the big-box stores and online retailers, Arlington's local business community is stepping up with unique offerings to help make this transition smoother—and more delicious.

Whether you're looking for a quick breakfast spot before drop-off, a place to celebrate that first day back, or services to keep your student organized and thriving, these five local businesses have you covered.

Division Street Diner

Start those early school mornings right at this Arlington institution on Division Street. Known for hearty breakfast platters and friendly service that gets you in and out quickly, it's the perfect spot for a pre-school power meal. Their pancakes are legendary among locals, and the coffee flows freely for parents who need that extra boost.

Rocketbelly

Texas' first self-serve boba bar has become a favorite after-school destination for Arlington teens. Located in the heart of downtown, Rocketbelly lets students customize their own drinks with endless flavor combinations. It's become the unofficial hangout spot for UTA students and high schoolers alike—perfect for celebrating that first week back or unwinding after a long day of classes.

The Tipsy Oak

When the first week of school is finally done, parents deserve a celebration too. The Tipsy Oak on Abram Street offers a family-friendly atmosphere with an impressive selection of craft beers and cocktails for the adults, plus a menu that keeps kids happy. Their outdoor patio is perfect for those warm August evenings, and the weekend brunch has become a post-school-run tradition for many Arlington families.

Frost Bank - Arlington Locations

Teaching financial literacy starts early, and Frost Bank's Arlington branches offer student savings accounts that help kids learn money management. With multiple locations throughout the city, including near the Entertainment District and downtown, they make it convenient to open that first account and start building healthy financial habits for the school year and beyond.

Arlington Public Library

Don't overlook this invaluable community resource. With multiple branches across Arlington, the public library offers free homework help, study spaces, and endless educational resources for students of all ages. Their summer reading programs seamlessly transition into school-year support, and the digital resources—including free tutoring and research databases—are accessible 24/7 with a library card.

As you prepare for the school year ahead, remember that shopping local isn't just about convenience—it's about investing in the community where your children are growing up. These Arlington businesses are owned by your neighbors, employ local residents, and contribute to what makes The American Dream City such a special place to raise a family.

Here's to a successful school year filled with learning, growth, and plenty of support from our amazing local business community!
