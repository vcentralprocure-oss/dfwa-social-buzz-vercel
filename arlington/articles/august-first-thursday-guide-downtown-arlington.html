Your August First Thursday Guide: Downtown Arlington's Monthly Block Party Returns

Live music, 3D chalk art, and extended hours at your favorite downtown spots—here's everything happening August 7th

August 1, 2026 Arlington Pulse Staff

Downtown Arlington comes alive during First Thursday events with outdoor dining and community gatherings. (Photo: Unsplash)

August is here, and that means one thing for Arlington locals: First Thursday is back. On August 7th, downtown Arlington transforms into a vibrant block party from 4 to 8 p.m., bringing together live music, interactive art, and the kind of community energy that makes The American Dream City special.

This month's highlight? A massive 3D chalk mural at Rotary Dream Park by renowned local artist Jan Riggins. Designed to celebrate the spirit of community coming together, this interactive floor-art piece is exactly the kind of experience that defines First Thursdays—unexpected, engaging, and totally Instagram-worthy.

Where to Eat During First Thursday

All that walking and exploring works up an appetite. Fortunately, downtown's best spots are extending their hours and rolling out specials for the evening.

The Tipsy Oak on Abram Street is always a First Thursday favorite. This gastropub-icehouse hybrid serves up chef-driven pub fare alongside craft beer and cocktails. Their patio fills up fast on event nights, so arrive early to grab a spot.

The Tipsy Oak

301 E Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

Gastropub featuring craft cocktails, local beers, and live music in the heart of downtown.

Visit Website →

For something more casual, Hurtado Barbecue on Front Street delivers Texas-sized flavor. Their Texas Twinkies have achieved near-mythical status among locals, and the brisket rivals anything you'll find in Austin. The outdoor seating is perfect for people-watching during the event.

Hurtado Barbecue

205 E Front St, Arlington, TX 76010

Award-winning Texas barbecue featuring brisket, ribs, and their famous Texas Twinkies.

Visit Website →

Craving something sweet? Rocketbelly, Texas' first self-serve boba bar, offers refreshing drinks to beat the August heat. Customize your bubble tea exactly how you want it—because after exploring downtown, you deserve something exactly to your taste.

Rocketbelly

200 E Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

Texas' first self-serve boba bar with customizable bubble tea and refreshing drinks.

Visit Website →

Live Music & Entertainment

First Thursdays wouldn't be complete without live music. Multiple stages pop up throughout downtown, featuring local artists across genres. Bring a blanket or chair and settle in at one of the performance areas, or let the music guide your stroll between businesses.

Don't miss the Levitt Pavilion, which often coordinates its programming with First Thursday events. This outdoor music venue has become the heartbeat of downtown's cultural scene, hosting 50+ free concerts annually.

Levitt Pavilion

100 W Abram St, Arlington, TX 76010

Outdoor music venue presenting 50+ free concerts annually in downtown Arlington.

More Local Favorites to Explore

While you're downtown, make sure to stop by Division Street Diner for their legendary breakfast-for-dinner options, or grab fresh tortillas at Marquez Bakery, a family-run institution that's been serving Arlington for decades.

Supporting Local Beyond the Event

First Thursday isn't just a party—it's a reminder of why supporting local businesses matters. Every dollar spent at a downtown spot stays in Arlington, supporting jobs and keeping our community unique.

Mark your calendar for August 7th, grab your friends and family, and experience downtown Arlington at its best. With live music, interactive art, and some of the best food in North Texas, First Thursday is the perfect way to kick off your August weekend.
